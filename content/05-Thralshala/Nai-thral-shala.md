<h1><span class="furling-pin">Nai'thral'shala</span> | The Wisdom That Moves Unseen Through Strength</h1>
<p class="rh-description">Intelligence work — knowing what the other side knows, protecting what you know from them — was not something we were well-prepared for when the Keth'alis War began. We had spent our history as teachers and partners, not as adversaries keeping secrets. The records here document what we learned, at cost, about the practice of security. Everything in this section is held at Citizen access and above.</p>
