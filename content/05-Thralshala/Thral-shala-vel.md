<h1><span class="furling-pin">Thral'shala'vel</span> | What the Study of Force Has Taught Us</h1>
<p class="rh-description">Furling military doctrine was built on a foundation that distinguishes it from most other species' approaches: we did not seek to win wars. We sought to prevent them, and when they came anyway, to survive them. The records in this section document how we organised defence, how we structured those who carried weapons, and what we learned — at great cost — about strategy. Everything here is held at Student access or above.</p>
