<h1><span class="furling-pin">Thevan'thral'shala</span> | The Wisdom of What We Built to Defend</h1>
<p class="rh-description">We built weapons reluctantly and documented them carefully. The documentation is held here at Student access and above — not because the knowledge is forbidden, but because weapons specifications without strategic and ethical context are simply instructions, and instructions without understanding are not what this archive is for. The philosophy of defensive technology is accessible first. The specifications follow when that foundation is in place.</p>
