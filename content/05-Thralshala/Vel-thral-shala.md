<h1><span class="furling-pin">Vel'thral'shala</span> | The Foundation of All We Know of War</h1>
<p class="rh-description">We have been in three wars. The first nearly ended us. The second cost us deeply but we survived it. The third we did not win. The records of all three are here, held carefully and requiring demonstrated understanding before they open. This is not because we are ashamed of them. It is because conflict records without context produce worse understanding than no record at all. Read the history first. The wars make more sense when you know who we were before them.</p>
