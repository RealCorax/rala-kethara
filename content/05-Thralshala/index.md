<h1><span class="furling-pin">Thral'shala</span> | The Knowledge We Wish We Had Never Needed</h1>
<p class="rh-description">The scholars who compiled this branch did so with grief, not pride. Several of them noted, on the record and as part of the record, that they hoped no one would ever need to read it. The archive does not share that hope. It shares only what was recorded, and holds the rest carefully.</p>
</div>

> [!selavai] Selavai
> I want to speak to you directly about this branch before you enter it.
>
> The archive was built on the principle that knowledge flows to all. This branch tests that principle. It contains the record of every conflict we were part of, everything we learned about defending ourselves, every weapon we were eventually forced to build.
>
> I am not hiding this knowledge from you. The index entries are all visible. You can see what exists and what each record holds. The contents are held at Deep Circle access or above, not because you are distrusted, but because this knowledge requires something underneath it first: an understanding of who we were before we needed these things, and what it cost us to build them.
>
> If you have read <a href="../02-Athelashala/" class="internal">Athela'shala</a> — our history, our philosophy, the Kora'thesh Veshúnar — you are already building that foundation. The records here will open as you demonstrate readiness.
>
> We were not warriors. We became, briefly and reluctantly, people who knew how to defend ourselves. There is a difference, and the archive tries to hold it.
