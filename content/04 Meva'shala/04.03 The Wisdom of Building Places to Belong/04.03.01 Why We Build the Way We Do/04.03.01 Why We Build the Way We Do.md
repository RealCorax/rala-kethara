# 04.03.01 Why We Build the Way We Do | Vel'skela'meva

*Compiled by Vel'shala Oriskan, Era 83. This record is held open to all visitors.*

There are no sharp corners in mature Furling architecture. This is not merely an aesthetic preference -- though it is also that. A sharp corner concentrates stress at a single point. An organic curve distributes it across the whole surface. The Furling understanding of how to build well began with the observation that living things do not have sharp corners, and that this is not an accident.

Nibiru was grown rather than built. The distinction matters to anyone trying to understand why the city feels the way it does. A constructed building is assembled from components. A grown building is a single continuous organism, shaped by patient guidance over decades, whose walls and floors and ceilings are not separate things held together but one thing in different regions. The warmth you feel in the corridors is not from heating systems pumping warm air -- it is thermal mass, the accumulated warmth of a structure that has been slowly breathing for 5,000 thara'keth.

## What Every Furling Building Contains

Every significant Furling structure, regardless of its purpose, contains three things.

A gathering space large enough for the building's regular community to meet. The Furlings considered the inability to gather as a failure of design. Buildings that forced people into corridors or external spaces for any communal purpose were considered incomplete.

Natural or simulated natural light reaching every inhabited area. Light matters to biology. A person who cannot see the quality of light outside -- whether it is day or night, clear or overcast -- loses their sense of time and place in ways that affect their health. Nibiru's artificial sun was not an aesthetic luxury. It was a requirement.

A private space for each individual. Community does not mean the absence of solitude. Every Furling building was designed so that each person living or working in it had somewhere genuinely their own -- somewhere to think without being observed or interrupted. This was considered as fundamental as the gathering space.

## Using Nibiru

The city's seven districts each serve a different knowledge purpose, but all of them offer accommodation. The housing clusters in each district are available to visitors. Personal quarters can be claimed on arrival -- the archive navigation system will guide you to available spaces.
