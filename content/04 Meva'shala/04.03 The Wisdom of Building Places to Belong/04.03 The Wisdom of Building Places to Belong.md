# 04.03 The Wisdom of Building Places to Belong | Skela'meva'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'shala Oriskan, Era 83. Revised Era 119.*

A building is a statement about what its makers believed people needed. The curved walls of Nibiru, the communal gathering spaces at every district centre, the personal quarters designed for both privacy and connection -- every choice made in this city's construction reflects a value.

This branch documents how the Furlings built and why.
