# 04.02 The Wisdom of Giving Life Back to the Living | Vetha'meva'shala

*Section compiled by Shala'vetha Kelomis, Era 83. Revised and expanded through Era 144.*

Healing is the most direct form of the second principle -- Compassion is Strength. The Furlings understood medicine not as the repair of damage but as the restoration of wholeness. This distinction shaped every practice in this branch.

Nibiru's medical facilities are located in the Vetha'Menara building in the Vetha'kela district. They are available to all visitors regardless of access level. The records here document the knowledge behind those facilities.
