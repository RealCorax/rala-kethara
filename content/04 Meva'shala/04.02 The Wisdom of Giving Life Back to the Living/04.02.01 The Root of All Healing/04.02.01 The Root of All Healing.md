# 04.02.01 The Root of All Healing | Vel'vetha'meva

*Compiled by Shala'vetha Kelomis, Era 83. This record is held open to all visitors. The Vetha'Menara medical facility in the Vetha'kela district is available to all visitors regardless of access level.*

The Furling word for healing is *vetha'meva* -- life-gift. This is not a poetic flourish. It is a precise description of what the Furlings understood healing to be: the act of returning to someone the life-capacity that illness or injury had taken from them.

This understanding had practical consequences. It meant that healing was not a transaction -- a service provided in exchange for something. It was an act of the second principle: Compassion is Strength. The capacity to heal and the willingness to heal were, in Furling understanding, the same thing.

## Nibiru's Medical Facilities

The Vetha'Menara -- the Life-Healing Centre -- is located in the Vetha'kela district. It is staffed by automated systems maintained by Nibiru's bio-organic infrastructure. These systems can diagnose and treat a wide range of conditions across multiple species biologies.

Visitors should know several things about these systems.

They will work on non-Furling biology. The cross-species medicine records developed over many eras of contact with other peoples are integrated into the facility's systems. If you are not Furling -- if you are human, Jaffa, Aturen, or any of the peoples the Furlings encountered -- the facility can assess and treat you. It may ask questions about your biology if it encounters something unfamiliar. Answer honestly.

They do not require payment. There is no exchange required.

They cannot treat everything. The systems are maintained but no longer updated. Conditions that were unknown to the Furlings at the time of the exodus may not be recognised. If the facility cannot help, it will say so clearly and direct you to the relevant records for human or alternative medical knowledge where available.

## The Furling Approach to Illness

The Furlings understood physical and psychological health as inseparable. A person's emotional state affected their physical healing. A physical illness affected their psychological resilience. The medical tradition reflected this throughout -- healers were trained in both, and the archive's medicine records are organised accordingly.

Mental and emotional wellbeing records are held at [[04.02.06 The Wisdom of a Mind That Is Cared For]]. These require Student access, but the Vetha'Menara facility can provide basic psychological support at Visitor level on request.
