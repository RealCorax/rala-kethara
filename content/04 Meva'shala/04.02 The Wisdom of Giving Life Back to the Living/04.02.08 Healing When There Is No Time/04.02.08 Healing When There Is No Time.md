# 04.02.08 Healing When There Is No Time | Vel'meva'thral

*Compiled by Vel'thara Noriskan, Era 131. This record is held open to all visitors because emergency situations do not wait for access levels.*

When there is no time to reach the Vetha'Menara, when someone in front of you needs help now, this record gives the essentials. It is intentionally brief. In an emergency, brevity is kindness.

## Immediate Response

Stop serious bleeding by applying direct pressure to the wound with whatever clean material is available. Do not remove the pressure once applied. Maintain it until the person can be brought to the medical facility.

If a person is not breathing and has no heartbeat, the Vetha'Menara can be reached from any point in Nibiru in under ten minutes by moving toward the Vetha'kela district -- follow the amber light markings on the floor, which activate when the building's systems detect a medical emergency in progress.

If a person is conscious but in pain, do not move them unless they are in immediate physical danger from their surroundings. Movement can worsen spinal and internal injuries.

## The Wrist Device

The wrist device carried by visitors from Phoenix Site includes a basic medical scanning function. Hold it near the person's chest. It will return a preliminary assessment of life signs and major injury indicators. This is not a diagnostic tool -- it is a triage tool. Use it to understand how urgent the situation is, then move accordingly.

## For Non-Furling Biology

The cross-species emergency protocols are integrated into the Vetha'Menara systems. If you are a different species from the injured person -- or if you are uncertain about their biology -- bring them to the facility and allow the systems to assess. Do not attempt species-specific treatment you are not trained in. The facility is better equipped than improvisation.
