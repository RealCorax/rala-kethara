# 04.05 Wisdom Given Freely to the World | Shala'meva'vel

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 83. Revised Era 112.*

Teaching is what the Furlings were. Not what they did as a secondary activity alongside science and governance and art -- what they fundamentally were, as a civilisation, at the deepest level. The third principle -- Knowledge Flows to All -- was not a rule imposed on the culture. It was an expression of something the culture had already decided about itself before anyone wrote principles down.

This branch holds the how of that commitment: the specific practices, methods, and frameworks the Furlings used to give knowledge to other peoples in ways those peoples could actually receive.
