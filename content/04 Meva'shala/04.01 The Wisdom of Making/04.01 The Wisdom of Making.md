# 04.01 The Wisdom of Making | Thevan'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'shala Kemiran, Era 79. Substantially expanded through Era 144.*

Furling technology begins with a question that is different from the one most technological traditions begin with. Most traditions ask: what can we build? The Furling tradition asked: what should we build, and for whom?

This is not a minor distinction. It is the difference between technology as the pursuit of capability and technology as the practice of care. Every tool in this archive was made for a person. Every system in Nibiru was designed for whoever would eventually need it. The bio-organic architecture of this city, the crystal memory systems, the portal network -- all of it was made with the understanding that it would outlast its makers and would be used by people who could not be consulted in advance.

That is why it still works.
