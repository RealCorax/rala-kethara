# 04.04.05 How Every People Feeds Itself | Vel'veth'meva

*Compiled across many eras by many contributors. Final additions Era 141. This record is held open to all visitors.*

Food is among the most personal and most universal of things. Every species the Furlings knew had food traditions -- what they grew, how they prepared it, who they shared it with, what it meant when they did. These traditions are held here because the Furlings believed that a people's food culture is part of their identity in a way that outlasts almost everything else.

This archive holds recipes, preparation methods, cultivation notes, and cultural context for foods from every species the Furlings had extended contact with. The records vary in completeness -- some represent decades of careful documentation, others a single meal shared with a contact team and noted with whatever detail was available at the time.

What is held at Visitor level is a selection of accessible recipes -- those whose ingredients can be cultivated or substituted in Nibiru's current facilities, organised by species of origin.

## A Note on Using This Archive

Recipes are not merely instructions. Each one in this archive includes notes on context -- what occasion the dish was made for, who taught it to whom, what it is meant to express. The Furling tradition of *meva'shala* -- gift-wisdom -- holds that a recipe without its context is diminished. Read both.

The Nibiru kitchen facilities are in the Meva'Kalara. They are available to anyone who wishes to use them. The archive system can retrieve any recipe in this collection on request.
