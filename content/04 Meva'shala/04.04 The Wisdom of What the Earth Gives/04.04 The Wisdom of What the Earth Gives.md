# 04.04 The Wisdom of What the Earth Gives | Mira'meva'shala

*Section compiled by Oma'vel Therishal, Era 72. Expanded through Era 141.*

The communal meal is one of the oldest Furling social institutions. Before they understood nutrition, before they cultivated crops, before they built anything that would last -- they shared food. This branch holds everything the Furlings learned about growing, preparing, and sharing it.

Nibiru's growing facilities in the Meva'kela district are operational and producing food. All visitors may use them freely.
