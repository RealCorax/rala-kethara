# 03.04 The Wisdom of How Others Speak | Rala'veth'shala

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79. Substantially expanded following the Four Great Races alliance. Final revision by Shala'vel Kemiran, Era 141.*

Language is not a tool for communicating meaning that already exists. Language shapes what meaning can exist. The Furlings, who spent 150,000 thara'keth learning other peoples' languages, understood that to learn how a people speaks is to learn how they think -- what they find worth naming, what distinctions they consider important, what concepts they have no word for because they have never needed one.

The translation systems in this city handle surface communication automatically. This branch holds the deeper records -- the full language studies, the structural analyses, the tools for genuine understanding rather than mere exchange of information.
