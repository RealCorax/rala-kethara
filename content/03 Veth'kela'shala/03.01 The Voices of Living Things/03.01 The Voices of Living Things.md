# 03.01 The Voices of Living Things | Vetha'rala'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 71. Individual species profiles added across many eras by dedicated contact teams. Final additions Era 144.*

Every people the Furlings encountered was given a complete record in this archive. Not a summary. Not an assessment of usefulness or threat. A record -- of who they were, how they lived, what they believed, what they made, and what they said when they thought no one outside their world was listening.

This was not merely scholarship. It was a statement of what the Furlings believed about other minds: that they mattered, that their existence was worth preserving in writing, and that the archive would hold their story even if they themselves could not.

Some of those records are now the only testimony that certain peoples existed at all.
