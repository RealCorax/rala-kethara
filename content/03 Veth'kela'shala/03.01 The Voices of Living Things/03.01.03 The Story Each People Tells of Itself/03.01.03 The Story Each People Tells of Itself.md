# 03.01.03 The Story Each People Tells of Itself | Veth'thara'rala

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79. Individual histories added across many eras.*

Every people has a history. Most peoples have several versions of it -- what they remember, what they have chosen to remember, what they have agreed to forget, and what was written down before anyone decided what the official version should be. The Furlings tried to hold all of these where possible.

The histories held at Visitor level for the nine principal species are summary records -- enough to understand who each people is and how they came to be where they were at the time of last contact. Full historical records are held at Student access and above.
