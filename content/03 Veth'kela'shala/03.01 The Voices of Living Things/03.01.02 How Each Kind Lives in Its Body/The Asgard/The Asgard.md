# The Asgard | Vetha'shala -- Asgard

*Biology record compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 71, drawing on exchange records from the Four Great Races alliance.*

## Overview

The Asgard are one of the Four Great Races, native to the Ida Galaxy. Small in body, immense in mind, deeply ethical, and facing a biological challenge of their own making that shadowed everything the Furlings knew of them in the later eras.

## Physical Form

The Asgard are small -- approximately 3 to 3.5 feet tall -- with large heads, large dark eyes, and minimal body mass. Their form had been modified substantially over their long history. By the time of the Furling alliance they reproduced through cloning rather than biological reproduction, a choice made so far back in their history that it had become simply who they were. The biological consequences of this -- a gradual degradation in the cloning process itself -- were the challenge that shadowed them.

Despite their small physical form, the Asgard projected considerable authority. They had earned it.

## Biology and Diet

Highly modified from their original biological state. Much of what might be called their biology had been replaced or supplemented by technology. Their brains -- enormous relative to their body size -- remained the central fact of their existence. They were, in the most literal sense, minds that maintained a body rather than bodies that contained a mind.

## Notes for Visitors

The Asgard were among the most principled species the Furlings knew. They held firm convictions about the protection of less developed civilisations and acted on those convictions consistently. Meeting an Asgard was to encounter a mind that had been thinking carefully about ethics for longer than the Furlings had been spacefaring.

Their sense of humour was dry and appeared unexpectedly. This was always a good sign.

As of the archive's sealing, the Asgard were working on the cloning degradation problem. Their current status is unknown and is a matter of deep concern.
