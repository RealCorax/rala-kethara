# The Aethen | Vetha'shala -- Aethen

*Biology record compiled by Vel'shala Kemiran and the Aethen Contact Team, Eras 89 to 112.*

## Overview

The Aethen are a species from Aethen'skela, a world that bears visible scars of a near-catastrophe that the Aethen brought upon themselves and then chose to step back from. They are, in the Furling understanding of the word, survivors in the deepest sense -- not merely of events, but of themselves.

## Physical Form

The Aethen are stocky and broad-shouldered, averaging 5 to 5.5 feet in height. Their build reflects a history as an intensely social ground-dwelling species -- they are physically strong and convey a sense of stability and rootedness. Their skin ranges from warm brown to deep umber, with subtle natural ridging along the brow and shoulders. Their faces are expressive, with large mobile features that communicate mood with considerable clarity.

Their hands are broad and strong. Aethen craftsmanship tends toward the durable and functional -- things built to last, to be used hard, to be repaired rather than replaced. Their world's surface, still recovering from what they did to it, is a constant reminder of what happens when things are not treated that way.

Lifespan is naturally around 100 thara'keth.

## Senses

Aethen hearing is their most refined sense -- they evolved in dense ground cover where sight was limited and sound was the primary means of navigation and communication. Their vocal range is broad, and Aethen music makes use of frequencies that other species can feel more than hear.

## Biology and Diet

Omnivorous, historically with a strong dependence on the large fauna of Aethen'skela. Following their civilisational crisis, Aethen dietary practice shifted substantially toward cultivation -- a deliberate choice to reduce their impact on their recovering world.

## Notes for Visitors

The Aethen carry their history with them in a way that is palpable. They are not a mournful people -- they chose to live and chose to change -- but they are serious. They do not waste. They do not take lightness lightly. They laugh, sometimes, at the right moments, and when an Aethen laughs it means something.

They are among the most reliable allies any species could want. They know what it costs to fail at being civilised.

As of the archive's sealing, the Aethen were establishing interplanetary capability. Their current status is unknown.
