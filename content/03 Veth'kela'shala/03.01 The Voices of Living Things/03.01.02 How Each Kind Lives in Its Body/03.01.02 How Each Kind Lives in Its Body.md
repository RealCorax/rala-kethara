# 03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body | Vetha'shala'rala

*Compiled by contact biology teams across many eras. Individual species records added as contact was established and maintained. This folder holds biological profiles for the nine principal species at Visitor level. All other species profiles are held at Student access.*

Each body is a history. The form a species takes reflects everything that shaped it -- the world it evolved on, the pressures it survived, the choices its ancestors made across billions of thara'keth of change it knew nothing about. Reading a biology record is reading the deep past of a people as written in their own flesh.

The nine principal species whose biological profiles are held at Visitor level are those the Furlings knew most closely: the four uplifted species of the Kigal Galaxy, the three other Great Races, the Athel'kin as they were before transformation, and the Keth'alis as they are now.
