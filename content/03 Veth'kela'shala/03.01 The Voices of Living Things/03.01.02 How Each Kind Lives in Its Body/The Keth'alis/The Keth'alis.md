# The Keth'alis | Veth'thral'shala -- Active Threat Record

*This record is designated for Visitor access because every person who arrives at Nibiru needs to understand it. It was maintained as a living document by the Keth'alis Observation Division from Era 138 onward. Entries are dated. The final entry is dated three thara'keth before the exodus. There are no entries after that date.*

*The complete biological, historical, and strategic records of the Keth'alis are held at higher access levels. What is held here is what every visitor needs to know to be safe.*

---

## Era 131 -- First Contact Record

*Compiled by Shala'vetha Kelomis, Athelveth Contact Team.*

First confirmed contact with the Athel'kin. Five Houses, pre-industrial, nomadic. Natural lifespan 30 to 40 thara'keth. Peaceful. Curious. Teachers by culture. Their healers -- the Ngo'verath -- demonstrate medical knowledge that warrants serious study.

Assessment: a people worth knowing. Recommend extended contact.

---

## Era 134 -- Formal Contact Established

*Compiled by Shala'vetha Kelomis.*

Full diplomatic contact with all five Athel'kin Houses. Received warmly. The Geirroh and Ngo'verath Houses in particular show strong interest in Furling knowledge. Teaching exchanges beginning.

The brevity of their lives is a constant presence in every conversation. Thirty thara'keth. Every elder who dies takes something irreplaceable with them.

---

## Era 138 -- The Vetha'meva Decision

*Compiled by Oma'shala Veriskan, Council Science Division.*

Following 7 thara'keth of study, the Council has approved the provision of longevity biotechnology to the Athel'kin -- the Vetha'meva, the life-gift. The therapy would extend natural lifespan from 30-40 thara'keth to 300-400 thara'keth. This is the same technology that extended Furling lifespans generations ago.

The decision was not unanimous. Dissenting voices are recorded in [[02.03.10 Where the Councils Certainty Ran Dry]].

The therapy was provided. The Athel'kin received it with gratitude that was difficult to witness without feeling the full weight of what we were giving.

---

## Era 139 -- Early Concerns

*Compiled by Vel'thara Noriskan, Athelveth Observation Post.*

Something is changing on Athelveth. The Houses have begun to conflict with each other in ways that were not present before. The Ngo'verath have withdrawn from contact. The Rrozok and Zrohga are in open dispute.

The longevity therapy is working. The Athel'kin now live long enough to accumulate grievances across generations in ways their 30-thara'keth lifespans did not permit.

Requesting increased observation resources.

---

## Era 140 -- The First Harvests

*Compiled by Vel'thara Noriskan.*

We do not fully understand what we are observing. A subset of Athel'kin who received the longevity therapy have discovered that something in the biological process can be -- redirected. Extended. That rather than the therapy simply lengthening their own lives, they can draw biological energy from other living beings to extend their lives further. Indefinitely.

The process requires the other living being.

We are trying to understand the mechanism. We are trying to understand how this was discovered. We are trying to understand whether it can be stopped.

The beings they are drawing from are not consenting. Many do not survive.

---

## Era 141 -- The Transformation

*Compiled by Oma'shala Veriskan.*

The group practicing what they are calling bio-energy harvesting is growing. They are no longer calling themselves Athel'kin. They are calling themselves Keth'alis -- the Eternal Ones.

The biological dependency appears to be permanent and irreversible. Once a person has used bio-energy harvesting to extend their life, they cannot stop without dying. They are trapped -- or they believe they are trapped, which produces the same behaviour.

They are not the people we knew. The teaching culture, the five Houses, the Ngo'verath healers -- all of it is being consumed by the logic of a dependency that cannot be satisfied without harm.

We should have stayed closer. We should have watched more carefully what that gift opened doors toward. This is the record of our failure to do so.

---

## Era 142 -- War

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Defence Division.*

The Keth'alis have attacked Furling settlements in the outer Kigal reaches. Three research outposts destroyed. Twelve Furlings dead. The harvest of subject populations is now operating at scale.

This is war.

Full military and strategic records are classified at Citizen access. What is documented here for all visitors is the essential fact: the Keth'alis are an existential threat. They require living beings to survive. They are expanding. They have no biological capacity to stop even if they chose to.

---

## Era 143 -- The Scale of What We Face

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh.*

Current Keth'alis population estimate: in the billions. Subject population under their control: in the trillions. The harvest rate maintains their immortality while consuming approximately one in ten of their subject population per year.

They have been doing this for five thara'keth. They will continue indefinitely unless stopped.

We are unable to stop them militarily. We have been unable to reverse the biological dependency. We have no cure. The Council is debating the only remaining options.

---

## Era 144 -- The Decision

*Compiled by Oma'shala Veriskan, Final Entry.*

The Council has decided to evacuate. The city of Nibiru is sealed. The archive is complete. What knowledge we could gather is held here for whoever finds it.

We are leaving the Kigal Galaxy. Most of us are going to the Milky Way, following the Alteran contacts. Some are going elsewhere.

We are leaving this record open to all visitors because if you are reading this in Nibiru, you are in the Kigal Galaxy. The Keth'alis control this galaxy. They have controlled it for approximately 4,460 thara'keth as of this archive's sealing.

What you need to know:

They appear as tall, graceful, humanoid beings. Beautiful, in a way that is slightly wrong. Their skin has visible bioluminescent pathways beneath the surface. They move too precisely. They are immortal. They are dependent on harvesting biological energy from living beings to sustain that immortality. They cannot choose otherwise.

They are not the people we loved. We grieve them. But they will harvest you if they find you.

The city of Nibiru is shielded. Its location is protected. The portal addresses in your wrist device do not include any address the Keth'alis know. You are safe here as long as the city remains undiscovered.

Be careful. Be quiet. And read the rest of this archive.

*Last update: Era 144, three thara'keth before the evacuation of Nibiru.*
