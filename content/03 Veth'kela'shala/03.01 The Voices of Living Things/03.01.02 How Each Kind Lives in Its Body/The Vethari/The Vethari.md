# The Vethari | Vetha'shala -- Vethari

*Biology record compiled by Shala'vel Oriskan and the Vethar Contact Team, Eras 51 to 79.*

## Overview

The Vethari are a desert-adapted species from Vethar'mira, a dry world of high plateaus, canyon systems, and fierce temperature variation in the outer Kigal Galaxy. They are practical, resilient, and extraordinarily skilled at working with their environment rather than against it.

## Physical Form

The Vethari are slightly taller than Furlings, averaging 5.5 to 6.5 feet. Their build is lean and efficient -- minimal mass for maximum function, an adaptation to a world where carrying unnecessary weight has a cost. Their skin is thick and heat-resistant, ranging from warm ochre to deep copper with subtle scaling texture that helps regulate temperature. They have a secondary eyelid that filters intense light and reduces moisture loss in dry air.

Their hands are remarkable -- long-fingered, with a grip strength considerably exceeding their apparent build. Vethari craftsmanship reflects this: their stonework and metalwork are precise, intricate, and built to last.

Hair is fine and typically kept short or covered in their home environment. Their hearing is tuned to low frequencies -- useful in canyon environments where sound travels strangely.

Lifespan is naturally around 120 thara'keth.

## Senses

Vethari have an exceptional sense of direction and spatial memory. Individuals raised in complex canyon environments develop a near-intuitive three-dimensional map of their surroundings that they maintain and update continuously. This same spatial intelligence drives their engineering excellence.

Their vision is adapted for bright conditions and distance. They can track movement at ranges that would challenge most species.

## Biology and Diet

Primarily herbivorous, with strong adaptations for extracting maximum nutrition from sparse plant material. Vethari digestive systems are efficient in a way that reflects a world where food is not always plentiful. On contact worlds with greater abundance they have adopted broader diets without difficulty.

Water conservation is built into every Vethari biological system. They require significantly less water than most comparable species and can tolerate levels of dehydration that would be dangerous to others.

## Notes for Visitors

The Vethari are direct and practical in manner -- they speak clearly and expect clarity in return. They find elaborate social ceremony tedious and are not known for patience with unnecessary complexity. Once their respect is earned, however, they are among the most reliable and loyal allies any species could ask for. Their word, once given, is kept.

As of the archive's sealing, the Vethari were establishing interplanetary capability and expanding steadily. Their current status is unknown.
