# The Shal'kora | Vetha'shala -- Shal'kora

*Biology record compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan and the Shal'vetha Contact Team, Eras 34 to 67. Reviewed and confirmed with Shal'kora scholars, Era 89.*

## Overview

The Shal'kora are an amphibious sapient species native to Shal'vetha, a world of extensive coastlines, shallow seas, and tidal wetlands in the middle Kigal Galaxy. They are among the Furlings' oldest friends.

## Physical Form

The Shal'kora are broadly humanoid -- bilaterally symmetrical, upright-standing, two arms, two legs -- but their adaptations to an amphibious life are evident throughout their form. Their skin is smooth and moisture-retaining, ranging from deep blue-green to slate grey with subtle patterning that varies between individuals. They breathe air but can hold their breath for extended periods and can slow their metabolism significantly when submerged. Their eyes are large and adapted to underwater vision as well as surface sight, with a transparent secondary membrane that protects against water.

Their hands have slightly webbed fingers -- functional in water, precise enough on land for the careful craftsmanship their culture prizes. Their feet are similarly adapted. They are strong swimmers and comfortable in rough water in a way that most land-adapted species are not.

Height ranges from 5 to 6 feet. Lifespan is naturally around 150 thara'keth, extended to 250 or more through the medical knowledge they developed in collaboration with the Furlings.

## Senses

Shal'kora have a lateral line system -- rows of pressure-sensitive receptors along their flanks that detect movement and vibration in water. This gives them spatial awareness in aquatic environments that exceeds any purely land-adapted species. On land this system is less useful but not inactive -- experienced Shal'kora describe an awareness of nearby movement and sound through it that is difficult to articulate in non-Shal'kora terms.

Their hearing is excellent. Their sense of smell is acute in water, moderate on land.

## Biology and Diet

Omnivorous. Strong bias toward aquatic food sources -- fish, shellfish, water plants. On land, fruit and cultivated grains are central. Shal'kora cuisine is among the most varied in the archive.

Reproductive biology: live birth, typically single offspring. Parental care extends for approximately 12 thara'keth. Young Shal'kora are born with stronger aquatic capability than adult form -- they spend their earliest years primarily in water, gradually shifting to more balanced amphibious life as they mature.

## Notes for Visitors

Shal'kora are warm, curious, and deeply social. They communicate with great expressiveness -- their lateral line system means they are aware of physical proximity and movement in ways that can feel intimate to species not accustomed to it. This is not intrusive; it is simply how they perceive the world. Meeting a Shal'kora who considers you a friend is one of the more pleasant experiences the Kigal Galaxy offers.

As of the archive's sealing, the Shal'kora maintained active civilisation and warm relations with all Furling-allied peoples. Their current status is unknown.
