# The Alteran | Vetha'shala -- Alteran

*Biology record compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 71, drawing on exchange records from the Four Great Races alliance beginning Era 56.*

## Overview

The Alteran -- also known as the Ancients -- are one of the Four Great Races and among the most technologically advanced species the Furlings ever encountered. Their origin is ancient even by Furling standards. They were among the first to develop spacefaring capability in this region of the universe, and they left their mark on the Kigal Galaxy before the Furlings had left Mira.

## Physical Form

The Alteran are humanoid and strikingly similar to the Furling form -- bipedal, bilaterally symmetrical, comparable height and proportion. This convergence was a subject of considerable interest to biologists of both species. The explanation appears to lie in the common biochemical substrate of all known carbon-based life rather than any direct ancestry.

Alteran physical appearance varies considerably. Their most notable feature from a Furling perspective was a quality of presence that older and more advanced individuals projected -- a sense of something held in reserve, of depth that exceeded the physical. Furling scholars who spent time with Alteran counterparts consistently noted this. Whether it was biological, psychological, or something else was never fully resolved.

## A Note on Ascension

The most significant biological fact about the Alteran is that they developed -- over many thousands of thara'keth of study -- the capacity to leave physical existence behind entirely, transforming into a state of pure energy and consciousness. They called this Ascension.

The majority of Alterans known to the Furlings had ascended or were pursuing ascension. Those who remained in physical form did so by choice, often to continue work in the physical universe that they considered unfinished. Their lifespan in physical form, extended by their technology, was very long -- the specific figures are held in deeper records.

What Ascension actually is -- what it means to exist as energy and consciousness without a body -- remains one of the most profound open questions in the archive. Records are held in [[07 Nai'shala]].

## Notes for Visitors

The Alteran the Furlings knew were unfailingly courteous and genuinely interested in other minds. They did not condescend, which -- given what they were capable of -- required a deliberate discipline they had clearly cultivated. Their friendship was one of the things the Furlings valued most. The portal network you use to travel this galaxy exists because of what they gave.

As of the archive's sealing, most Alteran had ascended. Their current status in physical form is unknown.
