# The Lumenari | Vetha'shala -- Lumenari

*Biology record compiled by Oma'vel Therishal and the Lumen Contact Team, Eras 67 to 94.*

## Overview

The Lumenari are a photosynthetic sapient species from Lumen'veth, an unusual world that orbits a binary pair of K-type stars. Their relationship with light is fundamental -- not metaphorically, but biologically. They derive a portion of their energy directly from it.

## Physical Form

The Lumenari are slender and tall, typically 6 to 7 feet in height, with a frame that is graceful rather than powerful. Their most immediately striking feature is their skin: a deep green-gold that shifts subtly with exposure to light. This is not colouration in the ordinary sense -- it is functional. The Lumenari skin contains photosynthetic structures that supplement their dietary energy intake. In good light, a Lumenari is noticeably more alert and energetic than in dim conditions.

Their eyes are large and adapted to the complex light environment of a binary star system -- they can resolve detail across a wide range of light intensities and distinguish light frequencies that most species cannot. In low light they have a faint bioluminescent quality to their skin, particularly around the face and hands.

Their hair -- where present -- is often gold or amber, and grows in patterns that maximise light exposure on the scalp. Some Lumenari cultivate it accordingly; others keep it minimal by choice.

Lifespan is naturally around 180 thara'keth.

## Senses

Lumenari perception of light is genuinely different from most species. They experience the spectrum of electromagnetic radiation more broadly and with greater discrimination. Colours that look the same to a Furling eye are distinct to a Lumenari. The practical consequence is that Lumenari art, when translated for other species' perception, loses something real.

## Biology and Diet

Omnivorous, but with a notably lower baseline caloric requirement than equivalent-sized species due to photosynthetic supplementation. In good conditions -- abundant light, varied diet -- Lumenari have exceptional stamina. In prolonged darkness, they become sluggish and eventually distressed in a way that goes beyond the psychological.

## Notes for Visitors

The Lumenari are contemplative and unhurried. They think carefully before speaking and mean what they say precisely. They find hasty decisions troubling. Their spiritual traditions are deep and elaborate, and they approach other species' belief systems with genuine curiosity and respect. They are not proselytisers.

A Lumenari standing in a beam of light -- which they sometimes do, quite deliberately -- is not being dramatic. They are eating.

As of the archive's sealing, the Lumenari maintained thriving civilisation and were considered among the Furlings' most thoughtful allies. Their current status is unknown.
