# The Nox | Vetha'shala -- Nox

*Biology record compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 71, drawing on exchange records from the Four Great Races alliance. The Nox provided limited biological information by choice. This record reflects what was shared willingly.*

## Overview

The Nox are one of the Four Great Races, native to a forest world in the Milky Way. They are among the most deeply peaceful species the Furlings encountered -- not through incapacity, but through a considered choice so old and complete that violence had become genuinely foreign to them. Their capabilities, when they chose to use them, were extraordinary.

## Physical Form

The Nox are slight and graceful, with an otherworldly quality that most species who encountered them found striking. They have fine features, large eyes, and hair that grows in elaborate natural formations they cultivate with care. Their skin is pale. Their movements are quiet and precise.

They live in harmony with their forest world in a way that goes beyond ecological sensitivity -- their relationship with their environment is intimate and mutual in ways the Furlings found profound and only partially understood.

## What Was Not Shared

The Nox did not provide comprehensive biological information to the archive, and the Furlings respected this. What is held here is what they offered. The deeper biology of the Nox -- how they achieve what they achieve, how their capabilities work -- is either unknown to the archive or held at higher access levels in response to Nox wishes. The choice is recorded here explicitly as a matter of respect.

## Notes for Visitors

Meeting a Nox is a quiet experience. They do not fill a room with presence. They are simply, completely, there -- watching with attention that feels like a gift. They do not judge. They observe, and they remember.

They will not help with violence. They will not be party to harm. This is not weakness. It is, the Furlings came to understand, the deepest form of strength they had ever encountered.

As of the archive's sealing, the Nox were thriving, peaceful, and largely withdrawn from galactic affairs by their own choice. Their current status is likely unchanged. They tend toward continuity.
