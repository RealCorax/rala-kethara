# The Athel'kin | Vetha'shala -- Athel'kin

*Biology record compiled by Shala'vetha Kelomis and the Athelveth Contact Team, Eras 131 to 138. This record documents the Athel'kin as they were at the time of first contact and through the period of Furling engagement with them. It does not address what came after. That record is held separately.*

## Overview

The Athel'kin were a peaceful, nomadic, pre-industrial species organised into five Houses, native to Athelveth in the Kigal Galaxy. Their natural lifespan was 30 to 40 thara'keth -- extremely brief. Every generation of elders who died took accumulated knowledge with them. They were perpetually racing against their own mortality, and losing. Despite this, they had built something remarkable.

## Physical Form

The Athel'kin were slightly taller than Furlings, averaging 5.5 to 6 feet, with a lean and graceful build. Their features were angular and expressive. Their skin ranged from warm golden-brown to deep mahogany, with faint natural patterning around the cheekbones and forearms that grew more pronounced with age.

Their hands were long-fingered and capable -- the Athel'kin were makers and artists, and their hands showed it. Their eyes were large and dark, adapted to the varied light conditions of a world with complex seasonal patterns.

Their hair grew in dense spiralling formations and was considered a matter of personal identity and pride. Athel'kin of different Houses maintained different traditional styles.

## The Five Houses

The Athel'kin were organised into five distinct cultural groups, called Houses, each with its own character and governance tradition:

The Rrozok -- the Ridge Walkers -- direct, efficient, strong leaders, skilled makers of tools and structures.

The Ngahzoka -- the Water People -- ceremonial, the keepers of oral memory, governed by blood lineage.

The Geirroh -- the Sky People -- open, intellectually curious, governed by collective knowledge.

The Zrohga -- the Fire People -- practical and survival-focused, leadership earned through demonstrated skill.

The Ngo'verath -- the Forest People -- quiet, observant, the most skilled healers. The smallest House and the most mysterious to the others.

## What They Built

In 30 to 40 thara'keth of life, the Athel'kin could not build what a longer-lived species could. But they had developed sophisticated tools, early metallurgy, written language, trade networks between Houses, and deep medical knowledge -- particularly the Ngo'verath, whose healing traditions were considered extraordinary even by Furling standards.

Teaching was their highest cultural value. The transfer of knowledge to the next generation before the current one died was not merely important -- it was survival. They understood this with a clarity born of necessity.

The Furlings loved them for it.

## A Note to the Reader

The Athel'kin described in this record no longer exist in the form described here. What happened to them is not part of this biological profile. It is part of another record entirely. The Furlings chose to keep these two things separate -- who the Athel'kin were, and what they became -- because both deserved their own telling, and because what they were deserved to be remembered without the shadow of what came after falling across every line.

They were remarkable people. That is what this record holds.
