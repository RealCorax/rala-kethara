# 03.01.01 The First Record of Every Living Kind | Vel'veth'rala

*Master index maintained continuously from Era 14 onward. Final update Era 144, three thara'keth before the exodus. The species listed here were known to the Furlings at the time of the archive's sealing. What has changed in the 4,460 thara'keth since that time is not recorded here.*

This record lists every sapient species the Furlings had confirmed contact with, observed, or received credible reports of by the time the archive was sealed. Each entry gives the species name, their homeworld, the nature of Furling contact with them, and their status as of the archive's last update.

For full records of any species listed here, follow the links to the individual species folders in [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body]] and [[03.01.03 The Story Each People Tells of Itself]].

## Species Known Through Direct Contact

### The Furlings
*Homeworld: Mira, Vara'skela system, Kigal Galaxy*
The people who built this archive. Their complete record is held in [[02 Athela'shala]]. Status at archive sealing: departed from the Kigal Galaxy following the Keth'alis War. Present location unknown.

### The Shal'kora
*Homeworld: Shal'vetha, Kigal Galaxy*
An amphibious species of the middle Kigal Galaxy. Peaceful, collaborative, with strong water-based engineering traditions. Contact began Era 34. Uplift assistance provided. Status at archive sealing: established independent spacefaring civilisation. Considered friends. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Shal'kora]].

### The Vethari
*Homeworld: Vethar'mira, Kigal Galaxy*
A desert-adapted species of the outer Kigal reaches. Practical, resilient, skilled environmental engineers. Contact began Era 51. Uplift assistance provided. Status at archive sealing: established interplanetary civilisation, early spacefaring. Considered friends. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Vethari]].

### The Lumenari
*Homeworld: Lumen'veth, Kigal Galaxy*
A photosynthetic species with deep contemplative traditions. Ecologically sophisticated, philosophically rich. Contact began Era 67. Uplift assistance provided. Status at archive sealing: established spacefaring civilisation. Considered friends. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Lumenari]].

### The Aethen
*Homeworld: Aethen'skela, Kigal Galaxy*
A species that nearly destroyed itself through civilisational conflict before choosing a different path. The Furling contact with the Aethen is among the most carefully documented in the archive. Contact began Era 89. Uplift assistance provided following the Aethen's own commitment to peace. Status at archive sealing: established interplanetary civilisation. Considered friends. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Aethen]].

### The Alteran
*Homeworld: Origin uncertain. Active in the Milky Way.*
One of the Four Great Races. The most technologically advanced species the Furlings encountered. Contact developed over many eras into deep friendship and scientific collaboration. Their gift of extended portal technology transformed Furling civilisation. Status at archive sealing: most Alterans had undergone a process of transformation to a higher state of existence. A small number remained in physical form. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Alteran]].

### The Asgard
*Homeworld: Othala, Ida Galaxy*
One of the Four Great Races. A highly advanced species whose civilisation was facing biological challenges at the time of last contact. Deeply ethical, deeply committed to the protection of developing civilisations. Contact developed through the Four Great Races alliance. Status at archive sealing: facing existential biological difficulties. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Asgard]].

### The Nox
*Homeworld: The Nox homeworld, Milky Way*
One of the Four Great Races. A deeply peaceful species who had chosen to live in harmony with their world rather than technological dominance of it. Their understanding of life and consciousness was distinct from any other species the Furlings encountered. Contact developed through the Four Great Races alliance. Status at archive sealing: thriving, peaceful, largely withdrawn from galactic affairs by choice. Full record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Nox]].

### The Athel'kin
*Homeworld: Athelveth, Kigal Galaxy*
A peaceful, artistic, short-lived people who were among the last species the Furlings offered assistance to. Their natural lifespan was 30 to 40 thara'keth -- extremely brief, which meant each generation lost much of what the previous one had built. The Furlings loved them. The record of what happened next is held separately. Full visitor record: [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Athel'kin]].

## A Note on the Keth'alis

The Keth'alis are listed separately from the Athel'kin because they are, in the most precise sense, no longer the same people. What the Athel'kin became following a specific biological intervention is documented in the active threat record held at [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body -- The Keth'alis]]. Every visitor to Nibiru should read that record. The Keth'alis currently control most of the Kigal Galaxy. They are the reason this city was sealed.

## Species Known Through Observation Only

The archive holds records of more than two hundred additional species encountered through remote observation, brief contact, or reports from other civilisations. These records are held in [[03.01.06 Those We Watched But Never Met]] and are accessible to Students and above.
