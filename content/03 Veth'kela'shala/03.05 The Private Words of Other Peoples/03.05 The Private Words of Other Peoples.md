# 03.05 The Private Words of Other Peoples | Rala'vel'veth

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 119. Substantially expanded through Era 144.*

The archive would be incomplete without these. Every species record in [[03.01 The Voices of Living Things]] describes a people from the outside -- what they look like, how they govern themselves, what they built. This branch holds what they said when they thought they were writing only for themselves.

Journals. Personal testimony. Letters. The private records of individuals whose names most outside their own people will never know. These are, in many ways, the most true records the archive holds.
