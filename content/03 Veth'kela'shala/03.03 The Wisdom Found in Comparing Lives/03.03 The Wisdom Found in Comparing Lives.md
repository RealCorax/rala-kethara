# 03.03 The Wisdom Found in Comparing Lives | Thara'veth'shala

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 94. Expanded by Oma'vel Therishal, Era 119.*

When you have known enough peoples, patterns emerge. Not superficial similarities but deep structural resemblances -- the same governance problems appearing in species that never knew each other, the same narrative shapes recurring in myths told across different galaxies, the same indicators of readiness appearing before a species makes contact. The Furlings studied these patterns carefully. They are held here.
