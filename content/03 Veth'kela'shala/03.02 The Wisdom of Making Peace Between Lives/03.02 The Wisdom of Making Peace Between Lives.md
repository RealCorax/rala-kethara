# 03.02 The Wisdom of Making Peace Between Lives | Oma'veth'shala

*Section compiled by Oma'vel Therishal, Era 88. Expanded by Vel'shala Noriskan, Era 112. Final revision by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 144.*

Every meeting between two peoples who did not know each other existed is a moment of extraordinary fragility. Everything that follows -- friendship, alliance, disaster, silence -- pivots on what happens in those first exchanges. The Furlings studied first contact carefully across every case they were part of, and across every case they could learn about from others.

This branch holds the record of every formal meeting, every uplift, every alliance, and every breakdown the Furlings were party to or witnessed.
