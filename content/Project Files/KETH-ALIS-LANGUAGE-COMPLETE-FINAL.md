## THE ALPHABET

### CONSONANTS — 17 sounds
*Ordered from deepest chest sounds to front of mouth*

| Character | Sound | How to say it |
|---|---|---|
| **r** | Chest growl-r | Rolled r from deep in chest |
| **ř** | Uvular trill | French r, back of throat |
| **ŋ** | Nasal back-throat | End of "sing" |
| **ñ** | Deep uvular nasal | Deeper than ŋ, more resonant |
| **g** | Hard palate g | "get" |
| **ǧ** | Growl-g | g with chest resonance behind it |
| **k** | Hard stop | "keep" |
| **ț** | ts sound | "cats" but harder and sharper |
| **z** | Voiced hiss | "zoo" |
| **s** | Unvoiced hiss | "sun" |
| **ž** | Growl-z | z with chest resonance behind it |
| **ʒ** | Zh sound | Middle of "measure" |
| **š** | Sh sound | "shore" |
| **ł** | Dark lateral | Soft Welsh "ll" |
| **v** | Soft lip-v | Barely lip contact |
| **h** | Soft breath | Pure aspirated h |
| **'** | Glottal stop | Middle of "uh-oh" |

*Note: z and s are natural partners — same mouth position, z is voiced, s is unvoiced*

## GRAMMAR

### Word Order
- **Active sentences:** Action — Doer — Thing
- **Descriptive statements:** Subject — State *(no verb needed — state is implied)*

Examples:
- *zok-roh · skar · ŋo* — "existence · not · us" *(statement: existence is not ours)*
- *ŋeva · roh · ŋo* — "give · life · us" *(action: we give life)*

### Verb Forms
| Tense | How to form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | Base form unchanged | *ŋoru* — speaks |
| Past | **ŋok** prefix | *ŋok·ŋoru* — spoke |
| Future | **ǧoh** prefix | *ǧoh·ŋoru* — will speak |

### Plurality
| Register | Method | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Informal / spoken | Repeat first consonant | *zok → zzok* |
| Formal / written | Add **-is** suffix | *zok → zok-is* |

### No Articles
There is no "the" or "a" in Keth'alis. Nouns stand alone.

### Stress
Always falls on the first syllable of a compound:
*keth-zara = KETH-za-ra*
*roh-ŋeva = ROH-ŋe-va*

## REGISTER DIFFERENCES

**Elite speech** — longer compounds, full formal Keth'zara titles, harvest discussed in terms of yield and administration. Always *roh-ŋeva* never *ŋeva* alone. Formal word inversions preferred.

**Working class speech** — shorter words, heavy idiom, *ŋeva* used constantly. Harvest discussed practically and directly. Keth'zara referenced as *zara-goha* rather than full *keth-zara-roh*.

**Military speech** — clipped, minimal, harvest in pure operational terms. Quota, yield, target. Little reverence language unless formally required.

**Street speech** — heaviest idiom, *ŋeva* used for almost anything of value, *ǧoh-zara* said constantly as a filler expression the way humans say "thank god."
