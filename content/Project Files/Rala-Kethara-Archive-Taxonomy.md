# Rala'Kethara Archive — Master Taxonomy
### *Shala'Thara'Veth — Wisdom-Tree-Living*

> *"To name a thing precisely is to understand it. To preserve it is to honour those who come after."*
> — Selavai, Rala'Kethara Archive

---

## Archive Provenance Note

This taxonomy represents the complete organisational structure of the Rala'Kethara Archive — the accumulated knowledge of the Furling civilisation across 150,000 years of spacefaring existence. It encompasses the natural sciences, the history and culture of the Furling people, records of every species encountered, applied knowledge across all fields, the complete record of conflict and warfare, creative and spiritual works, and all knowledge held in an uncertain or unresolved state.

> ⚠️ **The archive was sealed at the time of the Furling exodus, approximately 4,460 thara'keth ago. No new knowledge has been added since that point.** The 5,000 years following the exodus — including all events in the Kigal Galaxy under Keth'alis control — are entirely absent from this record. This is a known, significant, and permanent limitation until new contributions are made.

All documents may be cross-referenced across divisions. A document's primary filing location does not limit its connections elsewhere. Researchers are encouraged to follow threads wherever they lead.
