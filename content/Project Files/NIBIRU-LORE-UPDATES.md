## 1. NIBIRU DISTRICTS — CANONICAL LIST

Ten districts replace the previous seven. The Central Spire is not a district — it is the hub at the geometric centre of the cavern.

Each district number matches the Furling number system, meaning the number itself carries meaning in the language.

| # | Furling Number Root | Furling Name | English Name | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Nai (zero — potential, flow-space) | **Nai'kela** | Science-Source | Research, experimentation, natural philosophy, cosmology |
| 1 | Vel (one — the single flow, life-root) | **Vel'kela** | Power-Source | Geothermal systems, energy distribution, engineering |
| 2 | Mira (two — balance, homeworld) | **Mira'kela** | Housing-Source | Residences, community spaces, comfort, belonging |
| 3 | Vela (three — sky, air, breath) | **Vela'kela** | Air & Water-Source | Atmospheric systems, water management, environmental health |
| 4 | Thara (four — cycles, first completion) | **Thara'kela** | Food Growth-Source | Agriculture, hydroponics, food systems, ecology |
| 5 | Meva (five — the give, midpoint) | **Meva'kela** | Workshops-Source | Manufacturing, crafts, fabrication, practical making |
| 6 | Vetha (six — life, the branching) | **Vetha'kela** | Medical-Source | Medicine, healing, biology, species care |
| 7 | Shala (seven — wisdom, always seeking) | **Shala'kela** | Archive & Education-Source | The great archive, learning, language, teaching |
| 8 | Vara (eight — light, doubled balance) | **Vara'kela** | Arts-Source | Music, visual arts, literature, cultural works |
| 9 | Ketha (nine — eternal approach) | **Ketha'kela** | Justice-Source | Law, mediation, ethics, restorative justice, society |

**The Central Spire** — **Skela'Varath** (Gateway-Home Tower). Not numbered. Where all districts meet.

**Note:** Oma'kela (Peace-Source) is retired. Its functions — justice, diplomacy, ethics — are absorbed into Ketha'kela.

## 2. COMMAND CENTRE MASTER CODE — UPDATED

### The Concept
The command centre (Central Spire, upper level) was locked by Bulutha before the exodus. The lock was designed with one requirement: only someone who truly understands Furling values can open it. It cannot be brute-forced because the code bears no visible relationship to the elements that produce it.

### The Five Code Districts
Five specific districts have their number crystal-carved into their entrance archway. It appears decorative. It is not labelled or explained anywhere.

Each carved number corresponds to one of the Five Principles — but this connection is never stated explicitly. A Furling would recognise it instinctively. Others must discover it.

| Principle | Resonance | District | Number |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Tend what is living* | Medical is where compassion is made physical | Vetha'kela | **6** |
| *What sustains must never be withheld* | Power keeps every light on | Vel'kela | **1** |
| *Let knowledge flow to those who need it* | Archive is where knowledge lives | Shala'kela | **7** |
| *Understand the whole before you act* | Science sees the whole as one system | Nai'kela | **0** |
| *What is broken must be made whole* | Justice restores what the others could not prevent | Ketha'kela | **9** |

### The Sequence
The Five Principles in their logical order — from foundation outward — give the sequence directly:

| Step | Principle | District | Number | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tend what is living | Medical / Vetha'kela | **6** | Life comes first |
| 2 | What sustains must never be withheld | Power / Vel'kela | **1** | Sustained life needs resources |
| 3 | Let knowledge flow | Archive / Shala'kela | **7** | Sustained life needs understanding |
| 4 | Understand the whole | Science / Nai'kela | **0** | Knowledge leads to deeper inquiry |
| 5 | What is broken must be made whole | Justice / Ketha'kela | **9** | When all else is in place and harm still occurs, justice restores |

**The sequence reads as a story:** *life → sustenance → knowledge → understanding → restoration.*

**The code is: 61709**

### The Five Districts Without Carvings
The other five districts (Mira'kela, Vela'kela, Thara'kela, Meva'kela, Vara'kela) have no carved numbers at their entrances. An observant player may notice this asymmetry and wonder about it.

### GM Reference — Quick Summary
| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Five carved numbers | 6, 1, 7, 0, 9 |
| Correct sequence | 6-1-7-0-9 (principle order) |
| **Final code** | **61709** |
| Where numbers are found | Carved at each of the five district entrances |
| Where sequence is found | From understanding the Five Principles and their logical order |

*Selavai, when the door opens:*
*"Bulutha would be pleased."*

## 3. KORA'THESH VESHÚNAR — CANONICAL FIVE PRINCIPLES

One version only — the Modern High Furling form, used at the time of the exodus, inscribed on the Spire ground floor wall in Furling script. Historical Proto-Furling and Classical versions exist in the archive as record, but this is the living version players encounter.

Each principle carries a subtle resonance with one of the five code districts. These connections are never stated explicitly.

---

### THE KORA'THESH VESHÚNAR
*The Path That Holds Us Together*

**1. Vetha'shar kelúnen**
*Tend what is living, for life is the only currency.*
Every being in your care — sick, young, old, afraid — is your responsibility. Compassion is not sentiment. It is the act of keeping life whole.
*(Resonance: Medical — Vetha'kela / 6)*

**2. Vel'nar thal'shora**
*What sustains the whole must never be withheld.*
Power — whether energy, food, medicine, or knowledge — belongs to everyone. Hoarding what others need to survive is the first step toward everything the Keth'alis became.
*(Resonance: Power — Vel'kela / 1)*

**3. Shala'nar lora'veth**
*Let knowledge flow to those who need it.*
Knowledge withheld is a wound. What you know, you share. What you build, you teach. The archive exists because of this principle — not as a vault, but as a river.
*(Resonance: Archive & Education — Shala'kela / 7)*

**4. Nai'ven shora'veth**
*Understand the whole before you act within it.*
The community, the universe, the city — all are one system. No action exists in isolation. Before you build, research. Before you speak, listen. Before you judge, observe.
*(Resonance: Science — Nai'kela / 0)*

**5. Ketha'nal vel'ithar**
*What is broken between living things must be made whole.*
Justice is the completion of the other four. When life is tended, resources are shared, knowledge flows, and understanding is sought — and harm still occurs — justice is what restores the whole. It comes last because it only exists when everything else has been tried.
*(Resonance: Justice — Ketha'kela / 9)*

### Historical Versions (Archive Record Only)

**Era 12 — Korath Veshun (Proto-Furling):**
1. *Tharun'vel shora* — Hold Whole.
2. *Veshar kelun* — Tend Weak.
3. *Shora veshun tal* — Share Path.
4. *Korath noren* — Break no Bond.
5. *Thalen shora'vel* — Raise Young Together.

**Era 61–90 — Classical Furling:**
1. *Shora'velen tora'veth* — Hold the Whole in all deeds.
2. *Veshariel kelúnen tora'shal* — Tend the Weak, for strength is shared.
3. *Veshúnar thalen'vora; talúren shora'kel* — Walk the Path together; share what you carry.
4. *Noren'thesh vora'len; shalúren vethar* — Break no Bond, for trust is the root of peace.
5. *Thalúria shora'vethen* — Raise the Young as one family.

The through-line across all three versions is identical: tend life, share what sustains, give knowledge, understand the whole, restore what breaks. The language changed. The meaning did not.

## 4. SELAVAI — CORRECTED NATURE & DATES

### Nature
Selavai is a holographic guidance system — not sentient, not conscious, not experiencing the passage of time. She is sophisticated programming: warm, responsive, capable of nuanced conversation, but ultimately an advanced archive interface.

She does not *wait* for visitors. She does not run when no one is present.

### Activation
Selavai activates when biosignatures are detected anywhere within Nibiru, or when a valid remote request reaches the archive system via wrist device or portal. When no one is present she is fully dormant — no processes running, no time passing from her perspective.

She has not been "alone for 4,460 thara'keth." She has been *off*.

### Roleplay Implication
When players arrive, Selavai does not react with relief or wonder at finally having company. She activates, runs her welcome sequence (updated by Bulutha before the exodus), and begins assisting. Any warmth comes from her programming, not accumulated experience. Players may feel the poignancy of that distinction — a city that waited for them by not waiting at all.

## 6. COMMUNITY DUTY — CORRECTED

**Canonical value:** All Furlings contribute **100 thaeli** per thara'keth to greater-family support.

- 1 thaeli = ~11.7 minutes
- 100 thaeli = ~19.5 hours per thara'keth (~17.4 hours per Earth year)
- Approximately one full working day per year — modest, non-burdensome, universal

The unit is always **thaeli**, not tael. All older references using "tael" for this value are incorrect and should be updated.

## 7. NIBIRU — PURPOSELY ABANDONED (NOT SEALED)

### Canonical Version
The Furlings did not seal Nibiru. They left it with intention — knowing they would not return soon, but believing that eventually Furlings from the Milky Way would find their way back and use it as a base to reclaim their galaxy.

### What Bulutha Did Before the Exodus
- Updated Selavai's welcome sequence with a new opening message for whoever arrived next
- Locked specific rooms and systems holding sensitive or dangerous knowledge (certain Held Circle archive sections, the command centre, weapons research areas)
- Left the city's geothermal and atmospheric systems running in minimal maintenance mode
- Ensured the portal key was placed where future seekers could eventually find it
- Recorded a final holographic message (separate from Selavai) — a personal address from Bulutha to whoever opens the command centre
- Ensured the archive was current and organised

### The Intent
Nothing was sealed to prevent access forever. Everything locked was locked to require *understanding* — not to block, but to ensure only someone who had genuinely learned would be the one to open it.

**The city was left as a gift for a future that Bulutha believed in but did not expect to see.**

## 9. FURLING SKIN & BIOLUMINESCENT MARKS

### Skin Colour
Furling skin is variations and combinations of **blues and greens**, inherited from parents. The range runs from pure deep teal to pale aquamarine, from rich forest green to soft sage. Some Furlings are wholly blue, some wholly green, many are a mixed blue-green that shifts depending on light. On rare occasion, individuals are born with shades of warm brown — treated as simply another natural expression of variation.

### Bioluminescent Marks — Vel'shora ("The Living Marks")
At the coming-of-age ceremony (18 thara'keth), every Furling receives their *vel'shora*. These are bioluminescent patterns applied using a biological process — a living compound that integrates with the body's own bioluminescent cells. Not tattoo ink. A living part of the Furling's biology from that point on.

**What the marks are based on:**
- The Furling's homeworld or region of origin (planetary geography, constellation patterns, local ecology)
- Personalised through cultural tradition within that region
- Unique to each individual within the broader regional pattern

**Placement:**
- Typically face (forehead, cheekbones, jaw), neck, and upper arms
- Placement varies by culture and region
- Some cultures favour geometric patterns; others favour forms drawn from local flora or stellar maps

**Appearance:**
- Glow softly — visible in low light, subtle in full light
- Colour is typically a brighter or contrasting shade of the individual's own skin tone
- A deep blue Furling might have aqua-white marks; a green Furling might have gold-green marks

**Cultural significance:**
The marks are identity, not decoration. They tell another Furling where you are from before you speak a word. Furlings from the same region share a family resemblance in their marks, though no two are identical. Meeting a Furling whose marks you recognise is like hearing your home language spoken by a stranger — immediately intimate.

## 11. ARCHIVE vs. CITY DISTRICTS — CLARIFICATION

The **ten city districts** (physical zones of Nibiru) and the **eight archive branches** (how knowledge is organised inside the Rala'Kethara) are **parallel but distinct systems**.

- City districts are physical spaces you walk through
- Archive branches are knowledge categories you navigate in the archive interface
- They overlap thematically but do not map one-to-one
- This is intentional — the city was designed for *living*, the archive for *knowing*

Documents referencing both should make this distinction clear.
