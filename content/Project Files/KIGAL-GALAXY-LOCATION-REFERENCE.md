# KIGAL GALAXY — PLANETARY & LOCATION REFERENCE
*GM Reference. Covers confirmed facts, archive status, and open questions for all key locations.*

---

## FURLING HOMEWORLD — MIRA

**System:** Vara'skela (informal: Skela'el)
**Star:** Vara — K4V main sequence, warm orange-gold
**Full system data:** Complete — see Vara'skela System Data Tables

### Current State (GM knowledge — not in archive)
Mira was the heart of Furling civilisation for ~134,000 thara'keth. During the Keth'alis war it was invaded and largely taken. The Furlings knew they were abandoning it — there was no illusion of returning. All knowledge of Nibiru's location held on Mira was deliberately destroyed as the Keth'alis advanced, to ensure they could never find it.

Mira was not destroyed. The Keth'alis *farmed* it — Furlings are a sentient species, which means their bio-energy is harvestable. The Keth'alis extracted bio-energy from Mira's population during and after the war. Furling cities were mostly destroyed in the conflict, though not all — some structures survived because the Keth'alis needed infrastructure to manage harvest operations. The planet itself — its forests, its rivers, its extraordinary natural life — largely survived. The Keth'alis have no interest in destroying ecosystems. Managed worlds are more productive.

**Current status:** Under Keth'alis control. No sentient life remaining. The planet's natural ecology continues — the wilderness Furlings loved is still there, untended but alive. Furling ruins exist across the surface. It is used as a managed world, though with a diminished population after the intensive harvest of the war period.

### Archive Status
The archive records Mira as it was — in full detail, with love. City maps, ecological surveys, cultural records, the complete history of a living world. The archive does not record its fall. The last entries were made before the war reached Mira. There is no final note. The record simply stops, mid-flourishing, as if someone stepped away from their desk and never came back.

This is one of the most quietly devastating things a player can discover in the archive — the Mira section is rich, detailed, warm, present-tense in its voice, and then it ends. No war. No goodbye. Just silence.

## NIBIRU — THE HIDDEN CITY

### Critical Corrections (supersedes all previous documentation)

**Nibiru is not on moon M4S-457.** This was an error in earlier documents.

- **M4S-457** is a human SGC designation for a planet in the **Milky Way Galaxy**
- M4S-457 has a Stargate that connects to Nibiru — it is the entry point Phoenix used
- **Nibiru itself** is a city built inside a moon in the **Kigal Galaxy**, in the **Anu system**
- The Stargate on Nibiru is the *only* connection between Nibiru and the Milky Way

### The Stargate Situation
The Stargate originally on Nibiru connected it to the broader Furling portal and Stargate network. Before the exodus, Bulutha made a critical decision: the Stargate's location within Nibiru was moved to a secret position — noted only in Council-level archive records, inaccessible at Visitor level. This meant that anyone arriving in Nibiru through the portal dial system would not immediately find the Stargate, and anyone who found Nibiru through the Stargate network would have to search the city to locate the gate.

The address connecting M4S-457 to Nibiru was known only to a very small number of Furlings. All records of this address on Mira were destroyed before the Keth'alis took the planet. The address exists in Bulutha's personal records within the Nibiru archive — Council access only.

### Why Nibiru's Location is Unknown
The Keth'alis know Nibiru exists — it is part of their cultural memory as the Athel'kin, a legendary place they were taken to as students. What they do not know is where it is. No coordinates were ever given to visiting species. No gate address was ever shared. Mira's records of its location were destroyed. The only people who knew were Furlings, and they are gone from Kigal.

Nibiru is the most strategically valuable location in the Kigal Galaxy. The Keth'alis would do almost anything to find it — it contains Furling technology specifications they have never been able to replicate, records of species they would prefer to locate, and the archive of their own history before transformation that Draveth in particular wants suppressed.

### Archive Status
Nibiru's location is never recorded in the archive. The city's existence is not secret — the archive refers to itself and to Nibiru freely. But coordinates, system name, moon designation — none of these appear anywhere in the Visitor, Student, or Citizen layers. The location exists only in Council-level records.

## ATHEL'KIN / KETH'ALIS HOMEWORLD

### Two Names, Two Perspectives

**Furling name: Athelveth**
*athel* (ancient) + *vetha* (life) = "Ancient Life" or "First Life"
The name the Furlings gave this world when they first encountered the Athel'kin — warm, respectful, reflecting what they saw: a world of old and living things.

**Keth'alis name: Zok'ŋo**
*zok* (ground / existence) + *ŋo* (us / self) = "Our Ground" or "The Existence of Us"
Blunt, possessive, without poetry. Exactly how the Keth'alis name things. The contrast between Athelveth and Zok'ŋo tells you something about both species without requiring explanation.

The archive uses **Athelveth**. The Keth'alis use **Zok'ŋo**. Players encountering Keth'alis communications or captured records will see Zok'ŋo. Players reading the Furling archive will see Athelveth.

### What the Archive Records
The archive contains Athel'kin records from the contact period — who they were, their five Houses, their culture, their biology, the Vetha'meva. The archive notes that a crisis arose and that contact ended. Detail on what followed is locked at higher access levels.

The archive does not contain information about Zok'ŋo / Athelveth's current state under Keth'alis rule. Furling observers lost access during the war. The last records are from the golden age of Athel'kin civilisation.

### Current State (GM knowledge)
Zok'ŋo remains the Keth'alis capital — or more precisely, the seat of the three Keth'zara. It has been transformed over 4,460 thara'keth from the world the Athel'kin inhabited into something that reflects what the Keth'alis have become. The Furlings had limited information even during the war — intelligence on Zok'ŋo is thin in the archive.

## THE CHORATHI SYSTEMS

### What Is Known
The Chorathi are native to the **inner systems of the Kigal Galaxy's third arm**. They were already a multi-world civilisation when the Furlings encountered them — their expansion had been ongoing for several hundred thara'keth.

**The Chorathi boundary** is a clearly defined line established after the Era 56 war. Furling vessels do not cross it. Chorathi vessels are expected not to cross it in the other direction. The boundary held through sustained diplomatic maintenance — regular peacekeeping patrols, occasional escalation when Chorathi forces tested the edges.

### Planet Naming
Chorathi communication is harmonic — clicks, resonant frequencies, sounds produced by their Resonant caste that function simultaneously as language and social coordination. These sounds cannot be transliterated into Furling script in any meaningful way. The Furlings used **catalogue designations** for Chorathi worlds in official records, with a note that the actual Chorathi designations are harmonic complexes beyond written representation.

The Chorathi homeworld is recorded in the Furling archive as **Rethak** — an approximation of the primary harmonic frequency used by Chorathi Resonants to identify their capital, transliterated phonetically as closely as Furling script allows. The archive notes explicitly: *"This designation is an approximation only. The actual name is a harmonic pattern that requires Resonant vocal anatomy to produce. Rethak is used here for reference purposes and should not be understood as a translation."*

### Current Status
**Unknown.** The Furlings were at war during the final years before the exodus and lost communication with Chorathi territory. The archive records the last known state — a multi-world civilisation, caste-structured, with a clear territorial boundary — and notes that subsequent events are unrecorded.

What the archive does not know: the Keth'alis specifically targeted Chorathi Resonants to collapse their social structure. Whether the Chorathi survived this, adapted, or were broken is unknown from the Furling perspective. Players who want to know would have to go and look.

### The Chorathi Boundary — Navigation Warning
The archive includes a navigation warning for the inner systems of the third arm. It reads:

*"The inner systems of the third arm of the Kigal Galaxy are the territorial boundary of the Chorathi. This boundary was established by formal agreement following the Era 56 conflict. Furling vessels do not cross it. The Chorathi are not hostile by nature but defend their territory aggressively. The Resonant caste can coordinate a response faster than most species can mount a defence. Cross with full awareness of what you are doing."*

## THE VELHARI PROHIBITION ZONE

### The Incident
During the early expansion period (Era ~30), Furling terraforming teams began atmospheric seeding of a catalogued world. The planet's atmosphere was home to a distributed gaseous consciousness — the **Velhari** — which was not identified as sentient life until the terraforming process had caused catastrophic and irreversible harm to the vast majority of the population.

The Furlings attempted to render aid. The Velhari refused all contact and named the Furlings *"world-destroyers."* All communication ended permanently. The Velhari demanded the Furlings leave and never return.

The Furlings complied. Immediately and permanently.

### The Name
The Furlings have no name for the Velhari species beyond what little communication occurred before the end. The prohibited zone around the planet is marked in all Furling charts as **Vel'thala** — *"the guarded life"* or *"the life held apart."* This is a Furling name, chosen by the Furlings themselves to mark their obligation. It is not a Velhari name. The Velhari never provided one.

The planet itself has no Furling designation. The archive entry is brief — barely a page — because almost nothing is known and no further observation was ever conducted. The coordinates of the planet are recorded only in the navigation warning system, not in the general stellar catalogue.

### Archive Entry (Visitor Level)
The archive at visitor level contains:
- A brief note on the incident
- The prohibition — no Furling vessel is to approach within a defined range
- An acknowledgment that what happened was a Furling failure
- No further detail on the Velhari themselves, because none is available

The tone of the entry is one of genuine institutional grief — not guilt managed into policy, but an honest reckoning with having caused irreversible harm to a species they never even properly met.

The entry ends:

*"We do not know what the Velhari are. We know what we did to them. We hold that knowledge as a permanent obligation. The prohibition exists not because we fear them, but because they asked us to leave and we owe them that, forever."*

## THE ANu SYSTEM — NIBIRU'S HOST SYSTEM

The Anu system is the location of Nibiru. Beyond this fact, **no information about the Anu system is in the archive.** The location of Nibiru was withheld from the archive by design — providing system data would narrow the search space for anyone trying to find the city. The Anu system does not appear in the stellar catalogue. It does not appear in portal network records at visitor level. It does not appear anywhere a visitor could read it.

What the system looks like, what else is in it, what kind of star it has — none of this is documented in accessible records. This information exists only at Council level, and even there it is minimal.
