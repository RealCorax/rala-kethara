# 08.06 How to Add Your Voice to This Place | Meva'rala'keth

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 94. This record is held open to all visitors.*

The archive is not finished. It was never meant to be. A 150,000-thara'keth civilisation could not document itself completely even with dedicated effort across all those years, and it did not try to pretend otherwise. What it did was build a foundation and make the foundation extensible.

You can add to it.

## What Qualifies as an Archive Contribution

A contribution to the Rala'Kethara Archive is any record that:

Adds knowledge not currently held here. This includes events, species, technologies, histories, personal records, cultural works, and scientific findings from the period after Era 144 -- which is to say, anything you know that the Furlings did not.

Corrects an existing record where it is demonstrably wrong. The archive prefers honest correction to the preservation of errors.

Expands an existing stub or index with substantive content. Many records in this archive are held as shells -- the structure is present, the content is not. If you have the knowledge to fill one, fill it.

## Standards for Contribution

Every contribution must include: the name of the contributor and their species if known, the date of the contribution in the Furling standard time system or an equivalent that can be converted, the source of the knowledge being documented (direct experience, received testimony, documentary evidence), and an honest assessment of the contributor's confidence in the record's accuracy.

The archive does not require certainty. It requires honesty about uncertainty. A record that says "this is what I witnessed, I cannot verify whether it is representative" is a valid archive entry. A record that presents uncertain knowledge as certain is not.

## The Document Templates

The archive maintains templates for each document type: species profile, historical account, personal record, technical specification, creative work, recipe, and general record. These templates are available through the navigation system on request.

Ask the navigation system for the template that fits what you want to document. It will provide the structure. You provide the content.

## Access and Attribution

Contributions are attributed to their contributors. The archive will not anonymise a record without a specific request and a stated reason. The Furlings considered attribution a form of respect -- the person who knew something deserves to be remembered as the person who knew it.
