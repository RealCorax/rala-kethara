# 08.05 The Silence of Five Thousand Years | Nai'rala'athela

*Final entry added by Oma'shala Veriskan, Era 144, three thara'keth before the evacuation of Nibiru.*

This record was written knowing it would be read by someone who was not Furling, in a time the writer could not foresee, about events that had not yet happened.

If you are reading this, some time has passed since we left. We do not know how much. The last entry in this archive is dated Era 144. Whatever era it is now, the gap between that date and this moment is the silence this record names.

## What the Archive Does Not Hold

Everything that has happened in the Kigal Galaxy since Era 144 is absent from this archive. This includes:

The complete history of the Keth'alis occupation of the Kigal Galaxy -- their expansion, their internal conflicts, their governance, the civilisations that survived under their control and those that did not. The archive holds the Keth'alis record up to the point of the exodus. What they did next is unknown to us.

The fates of the Shal'kora, the Vethari, the Lumenari, and the Aethen. At the time of sealing, all four were alive, spacefaring, and considered friends. Their current status is unknown. We hope for them.

The history of the peoples who were living under Keth'alis harvest at the time of sealing -- approximately ten trillion beings on hundreds of worlds. Their suffering, their resistance, their survival. None of it is here. We grieve this absence.

Events in the Milky Way following the departure of most Furlings to that galaxy. The Alteran, the Asgard, the Nox -- their subsequent histories are not in this archive. What the Furlings who went to the Milky Way found, built, experienced, or became -- not here.

## What We Left Deliberately

The archive was sealed rather than destroyed for one reason: we believed that what we had gathered was worth preserving for whoever came next. We could not know who that would be. We left the induction materials open, the Keth'alis warning visible, the medical facilities operational, and the teaching resources available.

We left the deeper records locked not to withhold them permanently but to pace them -- to ensure that the knowledge most likely to cause harm if received without preparation would not simply be poured over whoever arrived without context.

## What the Archive Needs

If you have lived through the past 4,460 thara'keth of Kigal Galaxy history, you know things this archive does not. What happened to the uplifted species. What the Keth'alis built. How other peoples survived. What the resistance looked like, if there was resistance. What has changed and what has not.

The archive accepts contributions. The method is documented at [[08.06 How to Add Your Voice to This Place]].

The silence of five thousand years can be ended. That is why this city was preserved. That is why you are here.
