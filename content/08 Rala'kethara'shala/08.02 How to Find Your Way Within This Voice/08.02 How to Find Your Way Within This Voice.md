# 08.02 How to Find Your Way Within This Voice | Skela'rala'keth

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 94. This record is held open to all visitors.*

The archive is large. This record is a map.

## The Eight Divisions

**01 Thrala'shala -- What Moves All Things**
The natural sciences. Mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, and the planetary and stellar sciences. What the universe is made of and how it works. These are universal truths -- they belong to no single people.

**02 Athela'shala -- The Story of Our First Becoming**
The complete self-portrait of the Furling people. Their biology, 150,000-thara'keth history, society, philosophy, daily life, personal records, and language. Begin here to understand who built this place.

**03 Veth'kela'shala -- All the Lives That Are Not Ours**
Records of every other species the Furlings knew. Including the species you should know about for your own safety. The Keth'alis active threat record is held here at Visitor level in [[03.01.02 How Each Kind Lives in Its Body]]. Read it.

**04 Meva'shala -- Wisdom Made Into Use**
Applied knowledge: technology, medicine, architecture, food, and teaching. The most immediately practical division. If you need healing, food, or to understand how Nibiru works, this is where to look.

**05 Thral'shala -- The Knowledge We Wish We Had Never Needed**
Conflict, defence, and security. Heavily locked. The historical conflict records and the Keth'alis war records are held here. The Furlings are not proud of this division's existence but consider it necessary.

**06 Vara'oma'shala -- The Light That Lives Inside All Things**
Expression, belief, and culture. Art, music, literature, mythology, and sacred traditions -- both Furling and other species. The creative and spiritual record of everything the Furlings and those they knew made and believed.

**07 Nai'shala -- The Truth That Has No Name Yet**
The unknown and unresolved. Phenomena witnessed but not explained, questions held open, anomalies that do not fit existing frameworks. The Furlings considered honesty about the limits of knowledge as important as the knowledge itself.

**08 Rala'kethara'shala -- The Voice That Holds All Other Voices**
This division. The archive's record of itself. Start here if you are new. Return here if you are lost.

## The Access System

The archive holds five access tiers. Your current level determines which pages can be opened.

*Visitor* -- Automatic on arrival at Nibiru. The induction level. You are here now.

*Student* -- Earned by demonstrating understanding and respect for what the archive holds. The criteria for advancement are documented at [[08.06 How to Add Your Voice to This Place]].

*Citizen* -- For those who have committed to Nibiru as a home and demonstrated that commitment through significant contribution. The restoration of the command centre systems is one path to Citizen access.

*Keeper* -- For those who have earned deep trust through major achievement or sustained service to the city and its knowledge.

*Council* -- The highest trust level. Equivalent to Furling leadership standing. The full Council archive requires either decoding the Council chamber locks or earning Selavai's highest confidence over an extended period.

## How to Search

The archive navigation system can retrieve records by topic, division, access level, species, era, named individual, historical event, or keyword. Ask directly. The system responds to natural inquiry rather than formal command syntax.

Cross-references between records are woven throughout. A page in Division 02 that mentions the portal network will link to the relevant records in Division 04. Following these links is encouraged -- the archive rewards exploration.
