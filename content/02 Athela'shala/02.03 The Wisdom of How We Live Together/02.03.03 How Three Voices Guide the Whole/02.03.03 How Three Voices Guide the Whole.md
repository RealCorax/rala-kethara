# 02.03.03 How Three Voices Guide the Whole | The Council of 3 System

*Compiled by Oma'vel Therishal, Era 88.*

Every Furling-inhabited planet is governed by a Council of 3. Three individuals, making decisions together, guided by the Kora'thesh Veshúnar. No single member can act alone. No single member holds permanent authority. Decisions must reflect the principles -- not the preferences of any individual.

Mira houses the Head Council of 3, which oversees all planetary Councils across the galaxy. The relationship between planetary Councils and the Head Council is collaborative rather than hierarchical.

## Why Three

Why three? Because two can deadlock and one can dominate. Three voices guided by shared principles is the smallest number that can hold the Whole without any single voice breaking it.

The governance forms that preceded the Council of 3 all failed in the same way: they allowed power to concentrate. The Council of 3 resists this pattern. All three members must either fail or be compromised for the system to fail. The requirement to make decisions according to the Kora'thesh Veshúnar gives the Council a reference point outside themselves. And the rotating membership means no individual accumulates irreplaceable authority.

## What the Council Is Not

It is not a perfect system. The archive records many cases of Council decisions that were wrong, contested, or later regretted. What the system never produced was the kind of catastrophic personal power failure that caused Era 12. The Furlings concluded that this was sufficient.

## Qualification

Positions are not permanent. Members rotate. Qualification requires demonstrated wisdom, contributed service, and recognised standing within the community. It is not an inherited position. The Furlings understood the difference between being liked and being trusted. Council membership required the latter.
