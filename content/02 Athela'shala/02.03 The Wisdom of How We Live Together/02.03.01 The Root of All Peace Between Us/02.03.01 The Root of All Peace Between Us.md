# 02.03.01 The Root of All Peace Between Us | The Kora'thesh Veshúnar

*Compiled by Oma'vel Therishal, Era 88.*

The Kora'thesh Veshúnar -- The Path That Holds Us Together -- is the foundation of Furling social identity. It is not a legal code. It is not a religious text. It is the distilled answer to the question the survivors of Era 12 asked themselves: what must we never forget if we are to remain?

It exists in three historical versions, reflecting the evolution of the Furling language across eras. All three are preserved in this archive. The third -- Modern High Furling -- is displayed in every city centre, school, council hall, and off-world settlement.

## Era 12 Version -- Korath Veshun (Proto-Furling)

1. *Tharun'vel shora* -- Hold Whole.
2. *Veshar kelun* -- Tend Weak.
3. *Shora veshun tal* -- Share Path.
4. *Korath noren* -- Break no Bond.
5. *Thalen shora'vel* -- Raise Young Together.

## Era 61 Version -- Classical Furling

1. *Shora'velen tora'veth* -- Hold the Whole in all deeds.
2. *Veshariel kelúnen tora'shal* -- Tend the Weak, for strength is shared.
3. *Veshúnar thalen'vora; talúren shora'kel* -- Walk the Path together; share what you carry.
4. *Noren'thesh vora'len; shalúren vethar* -- Break no Bond, for trust is the root of peace.
5. *Thalúria shora'vethen* -- Raise the Young as one family.

## Era 121 Version -- Modern High Furling

1. *Shora'len isha'veth* -- The Whole is One.
2. *Veshúnar kelúra* -- Compassion is Strength.
3. *Lora'veth shalúren'ora* -- Knowledge Flows to All.
4. *Thesh'vethar isha'loren* -- Trust is Sacred.
5. *Thalúren shora'len'veth* -- Every Child is Ours.

## What the Principles Require

| Principle | Practical expectations |
|---|---|
| The Whole is One | Perform community duties. Support neighbours, elders, those who are vulnerable. Respond to calls for help. |
| Compassion is Strength | Care for the injured, young, and elderly. Offer guidance without judgement. Prioritise healing over punishment. |
| Knowledge Flows to All | Teach what you know. Contribute to archives. Mentor younger Furlings. Share discoveries openly. |
| Trust is Sacred | Speak truthfully. Honour agreements. Admit mistakes openly. Resolve conflict through dialogue. |
| Every Child is Ours | Mentor and protect all children. Teach through work and example. Ensure no child grows without guidance. |
