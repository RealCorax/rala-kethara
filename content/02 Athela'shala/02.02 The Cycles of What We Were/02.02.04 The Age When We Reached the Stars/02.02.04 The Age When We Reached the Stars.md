# 02.02.04 The Age When We Reached the Stars | The Golden Eras

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 131.*

Basic spaceflight began at the era marker 13:400:000 -- early in Era 13, approximately 400 thara'keth into the post-reconstruction period. The initial expansion was within the Vara'skela system. Full system exploration took approximately 400 thara'keth. Interstellar travel developed gradually over the following eras, aided in part by contact with the Alteran.

The Alteran had sent seed ships to multiple galaxies including the Kigal Galaxy. The Furlings discovered one of these vessels and were able to make contact. A friendship developed over many cycles. Through this collaboration, Furling portal technology extended from system-range to galaxy-spanning reach.

## The Teaching Missions

As Furling explorers and settlers spread across the Kigal Galaxy, they encountered other species at every stage of development. The response that became characteristic -- and eventually central to Furling identity -- was to teach.

The Four Principles that guided these missions: assess readiness carefully; give knowledge in layers; never give what cannot yet be received; leave the species more capable of helping themselves, not more dependent on Furling presence.

The four species the Furlings worked most closely with -- the Shal'kora, the Vethari, the Lumenari, and the Aethen -- each have full records in [[03 Veth'kela'shala]].

This era was not without difficulty. Some contacts were made too quickly. Some knowledge was given before the foundation beneath it was secure. The Furlings learned from these cases. What they learned, and the one time that learning came too late, is held in the records that follow.
