# 02.06.02 A Life Spoken in Full | Oral History Archive

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 119.*

Beginning in Era 89 and continuing through the final preparations for the exodus, Furling archivists conducted a systematic programme of recorded life testimonies -- extended conversations with individuals nearing the end of long lives, designed to capture the knowledge that lives in experience rather than in formal records.

The programme gathered testimony from working people and Council members, teachers and scientists, farmers and engineers, artists and healers. All were considered equally valuable. The archive does not believe that official lives are more worth recording than ordinary ones.

## A Representative Sample

Vel'thara Mevish, farmer, Colony World Rethas, age 387 thara'keth:
*I planted crops for three hundred years. I learned the same things from the soil every generation: it does not hurry, it does not judge, and it rewards attention. I tried to be more like soil.*

Shala'kela Orisven, healer, Mira Central District, age 442 thara'keth:
*In four hundred years of healing, I never met a person who was not also afraid. The ones who pretended otherwise were the most afraid of all. I learned to look for the fear before I looked for the illness. Usually the fear was older.*

Thara'vel Kelunir, archive keeper, Nibiru, age 289 thara'keth:
*The archive is not a library. A library keeps books. The archive keeps minds -- the patterns of how people thought, and why, and what it cost them. Every time you read something here, you are thinking alongside someone who is gone. I never stopped finding that extraordinary.*

The programme produced over nine thousand complete life testimonies. The navigation system can retrieve full testimonies by individual name, profession, world, era, or thematic connection to other archive content.
