# 02.06 The Living Voices of Those Who Came Before | Rala'vetha'vel

*Section compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 119. Final additions Era 144.*

An archive of a civilisation without the voices of individuals is a map without texture. Here are the individuals.

Every species record in other divisions describes peoples from the outside. This branch holds what those people said when they thought they were writing only for themselves. Journals. Oral histories. Letters. The private records of individuals whose names most outside their own people will never know.
