# 02.06.01 Words Written for No One but Themselves | Journal and Diary Collections

*Compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 119.*

The journal archive holds personal diaries, daily record books, and private notes from across the full span of Furling history. These were never intended as public documents. They were private records donated to the archive, or recovered after their authors' deaths, with the consent of remaining family members or by direct instruction left in the journal itself.

The Furlings did not consider private writing to be less true than public writing. Many considered it more true -- the self, observed without an audience, often speaks with greater clarity.

## Featured Excerpts

From the Journal of Thara'vel Kethis, Era 14, reconstruction period:
*Today I helped plant the first tree in the new district. It is a small tree. It will outlive everyone who planted it today. That is the point of it.*

From the Journal of Meva'lo Velashis, Era 73, during the first Shal'kora contact:
*We arrived at their settlement and they came to meet us in the water -- literally standing waist-deep as if it were a doorway. Tharan said they were being polite. I think they were being beautiful. Perhaps both.*

From the Journal of Shal'thara Omalin, Era 142, one generation before the exodus:
*The Council met for six hours today. I cannot record what was said. I can record how it felt: like watching a dam hold, and not being certain it would hold again tomorrow.*

From the Journal of Kel'thara Misaven, Era 149, five thara'keth before the exodus:
*My daughter asked me today whether Nibiru will remember us. I told her that Nibiru was built to remember. She said: but who will Nibiru tell? I did not know how to answer that. I hope the answer is: you.*
