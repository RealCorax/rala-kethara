# 02.05.06 Play Beneath the Stars | Games Recreation and Leisure

*Compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94.*

The games a people play tell you who they are in the moments when they are not trying to be anything at all.

The Furlings developed three games that spread across all their worlds and persisted across all eras.

Vel'shala is a team strategy game played on a circular board, in which players must build a complete path across the board while simultaneously disrupting their opponents' paths. Games can last several hours.

Keth'vel is a running and catching game with strong cooperative elements. Unlike most competitive games, the goal of Keth'vel changes each round based on a dice-determined condition. The flexibility teaches adaptability and prevents any one style of play from dominating.

Thara'vel is a storytelling game in which players collectively build a narrative, with each person adding a sentence or two before passing to the next. The formal version has rules about what kinds of contributions are valid. The informal version played by children has almost none.

Music is present throughout Furling daily life. Group singing is practised at meals, at work, at ceremonies, and during leisure. The concept of not being good enough to sing is foreign -- music is participatory, not performative in the sense of requiring an audience to evaluate it.
