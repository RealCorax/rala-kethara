# 02.05.04 The Tools We Made to Help Each Other | Tools Crafts and Everyday Technology

*Compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94.*

Every tool in this archive was made for a person. Every system in Nibiru was designed for whoever would eventually need it. This is not a coincidence of values -- it is the governing principle of Furling material culture.

The most distinctive class of Furling everyday technology involves engineered crystal -- not raw mineral crystal, but precisely cultivated structures grown to hold information, transmit energy, or function as bio-organic interfaces. The wrist devices carried by visitors to Nibiru are examples of this technology. The crystal matrix within them was grown over weeks to encode specific functions. The encoding is done through a process analogous to teaching: the crystal is exposed to information patterns repeatedly until it holds them.

Alongside the high technology, Furling daily life involved extensive hand craft: woven textiles, carved wood and bone, pottery, paper-making. These were not practised because the technology to replace them was unavailable. They were practised because making things by hand was considered valuable in itself -- a form of presence and attention that automated production cannot replicate.
