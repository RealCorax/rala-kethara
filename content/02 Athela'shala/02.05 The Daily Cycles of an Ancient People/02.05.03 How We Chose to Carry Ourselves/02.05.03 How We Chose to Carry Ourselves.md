# 02.05.03 How We Chose to Carry Ourselves | Clothing and Personal Expression

*Compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94.*

How a person chooses to present themselves is a form of language. The Furlings paid attention to what it said.

Furling clothing is characterised by simplicity of form and richness of texture. The typical garment is a robe or layered wrap in natural fibres -- cream, soft brown, muted green -- often with accent details in dark blue or dark red. The silhouette is flowing and unpretentious.

The choice of natural colours reflects Mira -- a world of amber stone, brown soil, green forests, and the warm orange-gold light of Vara. Even on worlds with different light and soil, Furling communities tended to maintain the colours of home.

Across all eras and all worlds, Furling clothing remained practical and non-restricting. The ability to move freely, to work, to respond quickly if needed -- this was never sacrificed for display. The most formal ceremonial garments are still garments you can walk in, kneel in, run in if necessary.
