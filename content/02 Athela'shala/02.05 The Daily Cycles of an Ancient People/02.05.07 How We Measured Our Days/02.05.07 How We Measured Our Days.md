# 02.05.07 How We Measured Our Days | The Furling Calendar and Timekeeping

*Compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94.*

Time is not a line the Furlings travel. It is a cycle they inhabit. The calendar is built from that understanding.

The complete measurement system is documented at [[01.01.07 Making Shape Useful]]. This record holds the calendar and its cultural meaning.

## The Four Seasons

Mira has four seasons, each approximately 100 mira'thara long.

Vel'keth -- First Light -- is spring. Warming, renewal, the emergence of new growth. Traditional planting time. Associated with new projects, new partnerships, new children.

Vara'keth -- Light-Full -- is summer. Full warmth and productivity. The period of greatest communal outdoor activity.

Thara'keth -- Long-Memory -- is autumn. Harvest, preservation, preparation. The season most associated with storytelling and the examination of history.

Kelun'keth -- Cold-Tend -- is winter. Shorter days, longer communal evenings. The strongest season for indoor gathering, music, and the making of crafts.

## The Time Units

| Unit | Definition | Approximate equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| tael | Base unit | approximately 1 second |
| thaeli | 700 tael | approximately 11.7 minutes |
| taelketh | 7,000 tael | approximately 1.94 hours |
| mira'thara | 84,000 tael -- one full rotation of Mira | approximately 23 hours 20 minutes |
| thara'keth | 34,440,000 tael -- one full orbit of Mira around Vara | approximately 1.12 Earth years |
| thara'athela | 1,000 thara'keth | approximately 1,123 Earth years |

The city of Nibiru operates on this cycle. The artificial sun above follows the mira'thara cycle.
