# 02.07.02 Voice Across the Eras | Language History

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79.*

Every era the language changed. Every era it remained recognisably itself. That is also true of the people who spoke it.

Furling linguistic history divides into three main periods. The pre-Era 12 period saw multiple distinct regional languages existing simultaneously, sharing common roots but often mutually unintelligible. The post-war convergence produced Classical Furling -- a cleaner, more explicit language designed by people who had learned the hard way that ambiguity causes problems. The expansion into the Kigal Galaxy during this period introduced hundreds of loan words from contact species that enriched the language without disrupting its structure.

Modern High Furling emerged gradually from Era 90 onward -- somewhat simplified compared to Classical Furling, but richer in vocabulary through 150,000 thara'keth of accumulated contact with other civilisations.

Modern High Furling is the language of this archive. All records generated by the navigation system use Modern High Furling phonological principles. The full grammar reference is at [[02.07.03 Modern High Furling -- Complete Grammar]].
