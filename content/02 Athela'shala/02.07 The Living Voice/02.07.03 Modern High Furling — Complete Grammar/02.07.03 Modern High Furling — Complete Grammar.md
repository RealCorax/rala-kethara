# 02.07.03 Modern High Furling — Complete Grammar | The Living Language

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79. Revised Era 112. This record is held open to all visitors.*

## Phonology

Furling uses a restricted consonant set -- exclusively soft and flowing. No stops, no hard fricatives.

| Furling | Approximate sound |
|---|---|
| l | English l |
| m | English m |
| n | English n |
| r | Soft rolled r as in Spanish |
| v | English v |
| h | Soft English h |
| sh | English sh as in shore |
| th | Soft English th as in the |
| k | Soft k as in silk, never sharp |
| s | Light, never a sharp sibilant |

Five pure vowels, always clearly pronounced. They do not reduce when unstressed.

| Vowel | Sound |
|---|---|
| a | ah as in father |
| e | eh as in bed |
| i | ee as in see |
| o | oh as in go |
| u | oo as in moon |

Stress always falls on the first syllable of any word. This is invariant.

The apostrophe marks a morpheme boundary -- a breath, not a pause or stop. The two roots of a compound are two banks of a river, and the apostrophe-breath is the water moving between them.

## How Furling Organises Reality

Most languages begin with an actor -- a subject who does something to an object. Furling begins with a relationship. A Furling sentence asks: what is the nature of what flows between two things?

*I see you* becomes *seeing flows from me toward you.*
*The star warms the world* becomes *warmth flows from Vara toward Mira.*

Basic sentence order: Relationship -- Source -- Destination -- Circumstance.

The flow particle *la* marks direction. *La'no* means flowing from me. *La'lei* means flowing toward you. *La'maha* means flowing between us.

The pronoun *no* (I) rarely opens a sentence. To begin with *no* is emphatic -- almost insistent. In ordinary speech, begin with the relationship, the quality, or the thing, and let the self appear as a participant.
