# 02.07 The Living Voice | Rala'vel'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79. Expanded through Era 141.*

A language is not a code for transmitting meaning. It is the shape of a mind. To learn Furling is to learn how we thought -- which is to say, to begin to understand us.

The language is not dead. It was spoken on the day Nibiru was sealed. The archive holds it living.
