# 02.07.04 Numbers Mathematics and Measurement | Numbers and Core Roots

*Compiled by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79.*

The Furlings counted in sevens before they counted in tens. The sky taught them. Vara and its planets: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven. Base-seven felt natural. Base-ten won eventually, standardised in Era 45 for consistency across worlds. Both were right.

## The Numbers

| Number | Furling |

| 0 | nora |
| 1 | vel |
| 2 | mira |
| 3 | thara |

| 5 | veth |
| 6 | shala |

| 8 | rala |

For larger numbers, compounds build logically. 11 is vel'keth vel (ten-one). 20 is mira'keth (two-ten). 100 is vel'mira'keth.
