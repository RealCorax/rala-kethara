# 02 Athela'shala | The Story of Our First Becoming

*Division compiled across many eras. Primary organisation by Vel'thara Oriskan, Era 79. Final revision by Oma'shala Veriskan, Era 144.*

This division is the complete self-portrait of the Furling people. It does not present us as we wished to be seen. It presents us as we were -- the full span of what we became across 150,000 thara'keth, including the decisions we are proud of and the ones we are not.

A visitor who reads nothing else in this archive should read here. Everything else the Furlings built and taught and believed grew from the foundations held in these pages.
