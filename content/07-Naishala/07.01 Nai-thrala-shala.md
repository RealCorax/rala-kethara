# <span class="furling-pin">Nai'thrala'shala</span> | What Was Witnessed But Not Yet Understood

<p class="room-subtitle">Observed, Not Understood — Everything detected and documented that does not fit current theoretical frameworks.</p>
</div>

<div class="room-intro">

This section holds every phenomenon the Furlings formally observed and could not explain. Not speculation about what it might be — the observation data itself, filed without interpretation, preserved for whoever comes next with better tools or a better framework.

The principle here is simple: an observation is not diminished by being unexplained. It is, if anything, more valuable. An unexplained observation is proof that the universe is larger than our current understanding of it.

Records in this section are filed with full observation data, the credentials of the observer, and the circumstances of the observation. Nothing is discarded because it is inconvenient. Nothing is reinterpreted to fit existing theory. What was seen is what is filed.

Individual records vary by access level depending on their nature. The section index is visible to all visitors. Specific records require Student access or above.
