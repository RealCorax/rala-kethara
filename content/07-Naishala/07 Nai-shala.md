# <span class="furling-pin">Nai'shala</span> | What We Do Not Yet Know

<p class="room-subtitle">The Unknown and Unresolved — Every open question, unexplained observation, and contested theory the Furlings formally preserved.</p>
</div>

<div class="room-intro">

Uncertainty is not a failure of knowledge. It is the frontier from which all knowledge eventually grows. An honest archive must distinguish between what is known and what is not. We preserve our questions with the same care as our answers — because a question honestly held is more valuable than a certainty dishonestly maintained.

This branch exists because we found things we could not explain, and we believed future minds deserved to know that. Some of those minds may be reading this now.

The contents of this branch are held at Student access and above. The branch exists. What it holds is there when you are ready for it.

</div>
