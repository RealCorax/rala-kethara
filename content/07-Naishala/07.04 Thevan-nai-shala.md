> [!selavai] Selavai
> "This knowledge is held carefully, not hidden from you. When you are ready, it will be here."
>
> Formally submitted speculative papers and hypotheses — clearly marked as unverified, preserved because today's speculation is often tomorrow's established theory. The Furlings did not consider speculation beneath the archive. They considered it a necessary stage in the life of any idea.
