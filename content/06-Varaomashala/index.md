<div class="rh-number">Branch 06</div>
<h1><span class="furling-pin">Vara'oma'shala</span> | The Light That Lives Inside All Things</h1>
<p class="rh-description">This is the branch that is hardest to summarise and easiest to spend a lifetime in. History tells you what we did. Philosophy tells you what we believed. This branch holds what we made — the stories, songs, myths, rituals, paintings, children's poems, and acts of devotion that were not required for survival and without which survival would not have been worth the effort. You will find us here more clearly than anywhere else in the archive.</p>

> [!arc-note] On Interpretation
> The works in this branch are presented as they were made. We do not interpret mythology as primitive science or creative work as cultural data. These are the ways we understood ourselves, celebrated existence, and made meaning in lives that were, for all their length, still finite. Read them as they were intended: as the real thing, not a record of it.
