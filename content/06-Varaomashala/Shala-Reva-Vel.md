# <span class="furling-pin">Shala Reva-Vel</span> | Archivist and Cultural Historian

> [!person-portrait]
> Era 100–148 · Mira, then Nibiru · Cultural History, Archive Curation, Cross-Species Arts

## Life and Work

Shala Reva-Vel arrived at Nibiru in Era 131 as a mid-career archivist with a background that was, by their own description, "accidentally broad." They had begun their working life studying Furling literary history on Mira, moved into cross-species cultural documentation when a research collaboration with an Aethen academic changed what they found interesting, and arrived at Nibiru specifically because the archive held the largest collection of Athel'kin creative work in existence and Shala wanted to understand it properly.

They spent seventeen thara'keth in Nibiru, becoming one of the principal curators of the Vara'oma'shala branch during its last period of active development. The visitor-level overview records for sections 06.01.01 through 06.01.03 were all compiled by Shala in Era 138, as part of a project to make the branch more navigable for visitors who arrived without any prior knowledge of Furling creative tradition.

Shala had a particular interest in how creative work communicates across species boundaries — what survives translation, what is lost, and what the losses reveal. Their approach to the visitor-level records reflects this: they consistently noted what could not be conveyed, alongside what could, on the grounds that honest incompleteness is more useful than false completeness.

They remained in Nibiru until the evacuation. They survived the transit to the Milky Way; subsequent records are not held in this archive.
