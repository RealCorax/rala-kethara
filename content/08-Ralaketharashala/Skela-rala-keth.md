# <span class="furling-pin">Skela'rala'keth</span> | How to Find Your Way Within This Voice

*This record is maintained by Selavai on behalf of the archive. It is updated whenever the archive's structure changes. The last structural change was recorded in Era 147, shortly before the exodus. No changes have been made since.*

>> This record explains how the archive is organised: the eight branches, the three access circles, and the practical mechanics of finding what you are looking for. If you are new to the archive, read this before exploring the branches. If you have been here before, the cross-reference table at the end may be useful.

## The Eight Branches

The archive is organised into eight branches. This is not a filing system. It is a map of how knowledge relates to itself the same way a living thing is not simply a collection of parts but an arrangement of parts that makes sense together.

| Branch | Furling Name       | What It Holds                                                          |
| ------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01 | [[01-Thralashala/index\|Thrala'shala]] | The universe - physics, chemistry, biology, astronomy, mathematics |
| 02     | [[02-Athelashala/index\|Athela'shala]]   | The Furling people - history, biology, culture, philosophy, language   |
| 03     | [[03-Vethkelashala/index\|Veth'kela'shala]]     | Other species - every people we have encountered                       |
| 04     |  [[04-Mevashala/index\|Mevashala]]          | Applied knowledge - technology, medicine, architecture, food, teaching |
| 05     | [[05-Thralshala/index\|Thralshala]]       | Conflict - war history, defence, security (mostly held at Deep Circle) |
| 06     | [[06-Varaomashala/index\|Varaomashala]]      | Expression - art, music, literature, myth, belief                      |
| 07     | [[07-Naishala/index\|Naishala]]          | The unknown - unsolved questions and unexplained observations          |
| 08     | [[08-Ralaketharashala/index\|Ralaketharashala]] | This archive - its history, structure, gaps, and what tends it         |

No branch is self-contained. A record about Furling biology links to records about chemistry, medicine, philosophy, and history. A record about the portal network links to physics, engineering, and the diplomatic record of the species who used it. Following the links is as important as reading the records themselves.

## The Three Access Circles

Every record in this archive belongs to one of three circles. The circles determine what you can read based on the trust that has been established between you and the city.

**The Open Circle** contains everything marked for visitor access. It is available to you from the moment you arrive. It is not a simplified version of the archive it is a complete body of knowledge in itself. The visitor-level records on Furling history, philosophy, biology, language, and technology are substantive and honest. Nothing in the Open Circle has been softened or edited to make it easier. It has been selected to give you what you need without requiring prior knowledge to interpret it safely.

**The Deep Circle** contains records marked for student and citizen access. These records require either demonstrated understanding of the material in the Open Circle (student), or active commitment to the city and its future (citizen). The boundary is not arbitrary. Some knowledge requires context before it can be held well.

**The Held Circle** contains records marked for keeper and council access. This is the archive's most sensitive material: classified historical records, weapons specifications, intelligence gathered on hostile species, the full unredacted record of council decisions. It is held carefully, not hidden.

Locked records do not disappear. Their titles remain visible throughout the archive. You know what exists. You can see the shape of what is held from you. The Furlings who designed this system believed that knowing a question exists is itself a form of knowledge, and that an archive which hid even the existence of certain records was dishonest in a way that mattered.

## How Access Levels Are Earned

Selavai manages access assessments for all visitors to Nibiru. The criteria are not tests or examinations in the formal sense. They are observations: of how you engage with what you have read, of whether you demonstrate understanding rather than mere familiarity, of what your intentions appear to be.

**Visitor** | granted automatically to anyone who arrives at Nibiru in peace.

**Student** | granted when Selavai observes that a visitor has engaged seriously with the Open Circle material, can demonstrate understanding of the archive's structure and the Furling values that underlie it, and shows no indication of harmful intent.

**Citizen** | granted to those who demonstrate lasting commitment to the city rather than temporary presence. The specific criteria are assessed individually.

**Keeper** | granted for significant service or demonstrated capability in matters of direct importance to Nibiru's function.

**Council** | the highest access level. Granted in recognition of trust equivalent to the trust the original Furling Council placed in its own members.

## How to Navigate

The archive has no single correct path through it. It has structure but not sequence  you may begin anywhere and follow the threads that interest you. The [[08-Ralaketharashala/index|branch index]] offers the eight doors. Each branch index then offers its sections. Each section contains individual records, linked to related records in other branches.

There is one suggested path for those who prefer to begin with orientation: the <span class="furling-pin-inline">Vel'thara</span> (First Path). It passes through the archive in an order designed to give each new section the context it needs. It begins here, in Branch 08, then moves to language in Branch 02, then to who we are, then to the universe we inhabit, then to what we have built. It is a suggestion, not an instruction. Leave it whenever something else calls to you.

Selavai is available throughout the archive to answer questions about navigation and access. She can be reached by speaking aloud from any location in Nibiru where holographic projection is active.
