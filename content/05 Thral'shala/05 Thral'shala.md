# 05 Thral'shala | The Knowledge We Wish We Had Never Needed

*Division compiled by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 131. Classification levels applied by Council directive, Era 143.*

The Furlings were not a martial people. The knowledge held in this division was accumulated because the universe did not always permit the luxury of peaceful existence, and because a people who refused to understand conflict on principle were simply less able to protect themselves and those they cared about.

This division exists. It is real. Much of it is locked.

The visitor record here is a simple honest statement: this knowledge was gathered reluctantly, held carefully, and is not the Furlings' proudest work. They were better at building than destroying. They are prouder of [[04 Meva'shala]].
