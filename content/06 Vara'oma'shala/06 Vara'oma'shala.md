<h1><span class="furling-pin">Vara'oma'shala</span> | The Light That Lives Inside All Things</h1>
<p class="rh-description">There is a kind of knowledge that cannot be made useful. You cannot apply a melody to a problem. You cannot solve anything with a story. And yet: the lives that included music, the evenings that included stories told aloud, the question of what happens after the last cycle — those were the point of all the practical knowledge. This branch holds what we made because we had to. Because we were alive.</p>
