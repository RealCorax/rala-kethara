# 06 Vara'oma'shala | The Light That Lives Inside All Things

*Division compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94. Substantially expanded through Era 144.*

The Furlings were scientists and builders and teachers. They were also artists and storytellers and singers. These were not separate identities. The same mind that proved a theorem wrote a poem about proving it. The same hands that grew a city's walls also wove cloth for no practical purpose, because the weaving itself was worth doing.

This division holds what was made. Not what was useful -- what was beautiful and true and worth making for its own sake. The art and literature and music of the Furlings, across 150,000 thara'keth. The art and stories of every people the Furlings knew. The mythological traditions that explain what happened before anyone was writing it down. The sacred practices that gave form to the things that cannot be proven but will not stop being important.
