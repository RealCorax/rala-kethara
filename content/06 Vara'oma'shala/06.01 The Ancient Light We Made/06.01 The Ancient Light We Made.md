# Vel'vara'shala

*Section compiled by Thara'vel Omashal, Era 94. Final additions Era 144.*

150,000 thara'keth of Furling creative work is held in this branch. It is one of the largest sections of the archive. The Furlings made things. They could not stop making things. Even in the eras of war and crisis, the making continued.
