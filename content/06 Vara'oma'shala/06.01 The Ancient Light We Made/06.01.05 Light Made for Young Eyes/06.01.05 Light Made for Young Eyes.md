# 06.01.05 Light Made for Young Eyes | Vel'vara'kin

*Compiled by Vel'thara Omashal, Era 94. This record is held open to all visitors, and particularly to any young people who may be reading it.*

Every culture's children's stories tell you what that culture believes matters. The Furlings told their children stories about: finding your way home when you are lost, the patience required to grow something, the courage required to admit you do not know something, the discovery that the thing you were afraid of can sometimes be approached and understood, and the particular joy of a meal shared with people you love.

They did not tell their children stories about defeating enemies. They told them stories about understanding things.

This section holds the children's literature, songs, illustrated stories, and play-oriented art created by Furlings across many eras. It is held at Visitor level for an obvious reason: children deserve access to it regardless of where they are in the archive's access system.

The works here include everything from the simplest illustrated picture-texts to more complex stories for older children. The navigation system can sort by age range, era, or theme.

## A Note to Adult Visitors

The children's literature of any people is its philosophy in simplified form. The Furling children's stories are not less true than the philosophical texts in [[02.04 The Ancient Wisdom of Living Peacefully]]. They are the same truths in a form that can be held by a mind still becoming what it will be.

Some adult visitors have found these records unexpectedly moving. This is not surprising. They were written by people who knew they were leaving, for children who did not yet know what was coming.
