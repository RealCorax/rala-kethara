# Vel'skela | You Are Home

*Skela'oma, venala.*
You are home, traveller.

Nibiru is a city of knowledge and welcome. You have been brought here because your people and ours have made contact, and because we believe that what we have gathered in this place belongs not only to us but to any mind willing to receive it honestly.

This is the Rala'Kethara, the Voice-Eternal. It holds the accumulated knowledge of the Furling people across 150,000 years of life among the stars. Science, history, language, other peoples, the things we made and the things we wish we had never needed to make. It holds our failures as honestly as our achievements.

You will be housed here, fed here, and cared for here. Nibiru has healers, communal spaces, growing gardens, and people who are glad you have come. You are not a guest to be accommodated. You are a visitor to be known.

Not all of the archive will open to you at once. Some knowledge requires a foundation before it can be carried well. We ask for patience, not because we distrust you, but because we have learned what happens when understanding arrives before it is ready. Your guides will help you move through what is here at a pace that serves you.

Much is open to you now. Begin where you wish.

*Selavai vesha la'leis.*
Selavai keeps watch. Ask her anything.

*Skela'oma.*
You are home.

## The Access System

This archive holds five levels of access. You currently hold Visitor access, the first level, granted automatically to all who arrive at Nibiru peacefully.

| Level | Who holds it |
|---|---|
| Visitor | Anyone who arrives peacefully |
| Student | Those who demonstrate understanding and respect for what the archive holds |
| Citizen | Those who commit to this place as a home and contribute meaningfully to it |
| Keeper | Those trusted with the city's deeper functions and knowledge |
| Council | The highest trust. Furling leadership standing or its equivalent |

Many pages in this archive are held at higher access levels. They are visible. Their titles and the names of the scholars who compiled them can be read. What is inside them waits until you are ready. Your guides can advise on how to progress.

## A Note From the Archivists

We have welcomed visitors from many worlds over many generations. Some arrived knowing nothing of us. Some arrived having heard stories they were not sure whether to believe. Some arrived with questions they had been carrying for years and had not found anyone willing to take seriously.

All of them were welcome here. So are you.

Begin with the philosophy. Everything else will make more sense for it.
