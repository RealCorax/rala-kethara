# 07 Nai'shala | The Truth That Has No Name Yet

*Division compiled by Oma'nai Veriskan, Era 119. Revised Era 144.*

This division holds what the Furlings did not know. Not the knowledge that was locked away for later, but the genuine unknowns -- the observations that could not be explained, the questions that remained open after 150,000 thara'keth of inquiry, the phenomena that did not fit any existing framework.

The Furlings considered intellectual honesty about the limits of knowledge as important as the knowledge itself. They did not fill in blanks with comfortable approximations. When they did not know, they said so, and they kept the record of what they had observed alongside the admission that they could not yet explain it.

The archive does not end where understanding ends. It continues into the uncertain, clearly labelled.
