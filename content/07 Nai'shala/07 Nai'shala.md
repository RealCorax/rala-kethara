<h1><span class="furling-pin">Nai'shala</span> | The Truth That Has No Name Yet</h1>
<p class="rh-description">Every archive lies a little by existing. The act of recording something implies that it is understood. Most of what we recorded was genuinely known. But we were honest enough to keep a branch for the rest. What is here cannot be summarised. It is, by definition, what we could not summarise. The titles are visible. You may know what questions we carried.</p>
</div>

> [!selavai] Selavai
> The records in this branch require Deep Circle access. Not because they are dangerous, but because they require a foundation of what we do know before what we do not know can be held productively.
>
> The section titles are visible here. You may see the shape of what we could not answer. That, too, is knowledge of a kind.
