# 07.02 Questions Without Answers Kept With Care | Nai'shala'vel

*Compiled by Oma'nai Veriskan, Era 119. This record is held open to all visitors.*

Some questions the Furlings kept open for 150,000 thara'keth without resolving. This is not failure. Some questions are not yet answerable with available methods. Some may not be answerable at all. The Furlings considered it important to keep those questions visible rather than burying them under comfortable approximations.

A selection of the longest-held open questions:

How did life first arise from non-living chemistry? The conditions are known. The chemistry is understood. The specific steps by which a self-replicating system first emerged from non-replicating precursors remain unknown. The question has been open since Era 44.

What is consciousness? The Furlings could describe neural correlates of awareness. They could not explain why any physical process gives rise to the experience of being inside it. This question is held in [[02.04.05 Who We Are When Nothing Defines Us]] at Student access.

Are the Furlings alone in having made the choice they made -- to teach rather than to dominate? The archive holds records of many species. Not all of them are still here. The question of what determined which civilisations chose connection and which chose conquest is genuinely open.

What is Ascension? The Alteran achieved it. They did not fully explain it. The records of what they shared are held in the deeper records of this archive.

What happened to the Furlings who went elsewhere? Not all of them followed the main group to the Milky Way. The records of those departures are held at Keeper access. Whether they survived, and where they are now, is unknown.

These questions belong to whoever is willing to keep working on them. The archive holds what was known. What comes next is yours.
