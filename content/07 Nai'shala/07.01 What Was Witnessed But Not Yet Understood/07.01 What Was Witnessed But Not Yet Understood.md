# 07.01 What Was Witnessed But Not Yet Understood | Nai'vel'shala

*Compiled by Oma'nai Veriskan, Era 119. This record is held open to all visitors.*

The following observations are confirmed as real -- multiple witnesses, repeatable conditions where possible, careful documentation -- but not yet explained by any framework in the archive. They are listed here because the archive does not pretend the universe is simpler than it is.

The list is not exhaustive. It is a selected representation of the kinds of anomalies held in this branch.

A crystalline formation found on Naiveth that encodes information using a system that resembles no known Furling, Alteran, Asgard, or Nox encoding method. It may be a natural formation. It may not be. It has not been decoded.

Seventeen separate observations of a particular navigational anomaly in the outer Kigal Galaxy where portal exits emerge shifted several light-seconds from their calculated arrival point. The cause has not been found. The locations are marked and the anomaly is documented. Travellers who receive portal addresses in the outer Kigal should be aware of this.

The Nox demonstrated capabilities in several documented encounters that have no physical explanation within the frameworks the Furlings understood. The Nox chose not to explain them. The Furlings respected this. The observations are recorded without explanation.

The full anomaly index is accessible here. Each entry is documented with the date of observation, the observer's credentials, the conditions, and the current status of attempts to explain it.
