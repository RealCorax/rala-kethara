# 01.01.07 Making Shape Useful | Vel'thael'meva

*Unit definitions established by Varathal, Era 14. Standardised to base-10 by the Council of Sciences, Era 45. This record is held open to all visitors as the reference standard for all measurements throughout the archive.*

Every measurement in this archive is expressed in Furling standard units. Those units are anchored to Mira -- to the world that gave us life, its size, its weight, its warmth, its rhythms. This was not imprecision. It was a philosophical choice that has held for over 140,000 thara'keth without revision.

The argument, made by Varathal when establishing the stellar reference standard in Era 14, was this: a measurement system anchored to abstractions is a system that belongs to no one. A measurement system anchored to the world you stand on belongs to everyone who has ever stood on it. Every Furling who learned these units did so knowing that the shael was the span of their own arms. That the lath was the temperature at which their world's oceans began to freeze. That the thara'keth was the time it took their world to circle their star once. The units carried Mira inside them.

For those arriving from worlds with different measurement systems, the approximate equivalents given here should allow translation.

## Length

The *shael* is the primary unit of length.

It was originally defined as the arm-span of a standing Furling adult -- the distance from fingertip to fingertip with arms fully extended. It was later standardised against a specific light emission frequency from crystal-lattice technology, making it reproducible by any civilisation with access to Furling materials.

One shael is approximately 0.925 of the unit humans call a metre.

The *shelai* cubed is the primary unit of volume.

The *shael'var* is used for large distances within a star system. See orbital distances below.

## Mass

The *thal'keth* is the primary unit of mass.

Derived from *thrala* (force) and *keth* (enduring). The Furlings understood mass not as weight but as that which persists -- consistent with their view of the universe as cyclic rather than linear. The thal'keth is defined as one ten-billionth of Mira's total mass.

One thal'keth is approximately 2.08 times ten to the power of fourteen of the unit humans call a kilogram. For practical everyday use, fractions of the thal'keth are standard.

## Temperature

The *lath* is the primary unit of temperature.

It is one of the oldest Furling units, predating formal language standardisation. The root *la* means warmth. Zero lath is the temperature at which Mira's deep oceans begin to freeze solid -- the point below which Mira's life-sustaining systems fail. The scale is life-centred by design.

| Reference point | Lath value |
|---|---|
| Deep ocean freeze | 0 lath |
| Surface water freeze | ~37 lath |
| Mira global average | ~100 lath |
| Comfortable Furling ambient | ~110 to 120 lath |
| Warm summer maximum on Mira | ~160 lath |

One lath is approximately 0.35 of a degree Celsius in relative terms. Zero lath corresponds to approximately negative 100 degrees Celsius.

## Time

The Furling time system is built from the *tael* upward. The word *tael* contracted from *thara* (cycles) over many generations of spoken use. Time is understood as nested cycles, not a line.

| Unit | Definition | Approximate equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| tael | Base unit | approximately 1 second |
| thaeli | 700 tael | approximately 11.7 minutes |
| taelketh | 7,000 tael | approximately 1.94 hours |
| mira'thara | 84,000 tael -- one full rotation of Mira | approximately 23 hours 20 minutes |
| thara'keth | 34,440,000 tael -- one full orbit of Mira around Vara | approximately 1.12 Earth years |
| thara'vel | 10 thara'keth | approximately 11.2 Earth years |
| thara'shala | 100 thara'keth | approximately 112 Earth years |
| thara'athela | 1,000 thara'keth | approximately 1,123 Earth years |
| tael'kora | 1,000,000 thara'keth | approximately 1.12 billion Earth years |

One mira'thara is divided into 12 taelketh. This is the standard Furling clock. The city of Nibiru operates on this cycle.

## Orbital Distance

The *skela'var* is the unit of orbital distance. Derived from *skela* (home) and *var* (star/light) -- home-to-star. It is defined as Mira's mean orbital distance from Vara.

| Body | Distance |
|---|---|
| Vareth | 0.33 skela'var |
| Thrael | 0.59 skela'var |
| Mira | 1.00 skela'var (by definition) |
| Velakris belt | approximately 1.39 skela'var |
| Naiveth | 2.04 skela'var |
| Thalavar | 5.19 skela'var |
| Velathara disk | approximately 22 skela'var |

## Stellar Reference Units

When measuring other stars, Vara itself is the reference standard. All values of 1.0 represent Vara's own measured quantity.

| Unit | What it measures |
|---|---|
| vara'keth | Stellar mass |
| vara'shael | Stellar radius |
| vara'val | Stellar luminosity |

## Pressure and Gravity

The *vela'thrala* is the unit of atmospheric pressure, defined as Mira's mean sea-level pressure. Worlds with no atmosphere register zero vela'thrala. Mira is 1.0 by definition.

The *mira'thrala* is the unit of surface gravity, defined as Mira's own surface gravitational pull. All surface gravity readings are expressed as a ratio to this standard.
