# 01.04 The Wisdom of Force Within All Living Things | Vetha'vel'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Kelomis, Era 44. Substantially expanded by Shala'vetha Oriskan, Era 97. Revised through Era 143.*

Something in this universe has the property of being alive. It is not a simple property -- no single feature defines it, no clear line separates the living from the not-living at every edge case. But the living things we know share a recognisable cluster of qualities: they take in matter and energy, they maintain their internal order against the tendency of everything toward disorder, they respond to their surroundings, and they produce more of themselves.

The Furlings studied life from two directions, as they studied chemistry: from the theoretical inward, asking what life was made of and how its mechanisms worked; and from the practical outward, as healers and farmers and ecologists accumulated knowledge of what living things did and needed.

What they found was not only the mechanisms of life on Mira. They found, when they reached other worlds and encountered other living things, that life across the Kigal Galaxy shares a deep common substrate. The same chemistry. The same basic information storage. The same fundamental logic of reproduction and variation and selection. Life, wherever the Furlings found it, was recognisably related to the same underlying principles.

That convergence is one of the most important findings in this archive.
