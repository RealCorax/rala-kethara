# 01.05 The Wisdom of Where Worlds and Stars Live | Skela'vel'shala

*Section compiled by Varathal, Era 14. Expanded by Omavel, Era 91. Substantially revised by Shalaveth, Era 112. Final additions by Vel'thara Omashal, Era 144.*

The universe is mostly empty. Between the stars there is almost nothing -- a scattering of particles so thin it would be considered a near-perfect vacuum by the standards of any world's surface. Worlds are rare. Worlds that hold life are rarer still. Worlds that hold life capable of looking up and asking where it is -- rarest of all.

This branch holds everything the Furlings learned about the physical homes of living things: how stars are born and how they age, how worlds form around them, what makes a world capable of sustaining life across billions of thara'keth, and how that life reshapes its world in return. It holds the complete record of the Vara'skela system -- the star system of the Furling homeworld -- and surveys of every system the Furlings visited.

Mira and Vara are the best-documented world and star in the archive. They serve as the reference standards against which all other worlds and stars are measured. For any visitor trying to understand where Nibiru's systems come from and why they work the way they do, these records are essential.
