# 01.02 The Wisdom of Force Upon Living Things | Thral'vel'shala

*Section compiled by Vel'thara Mevashis, Era 31. Expanded across many eras. Final revision by Thara'shala Kelisometh, Era 138.*

Physics is the study of force -- of how things move and resist movement, how energy travels and transforms, how matter holds together and sometimes does not. It is the oldest of the formal sciences in the Furling tradition, arising from the practical needs of builders and farmers long before it was understood as a discipline in its own right.

The Furlings eventually understood that all the forces they had studied separately -- the pull of the ground, the warmth of Vara, the movement of light, the hold of the smallest pieces of matter -- are aspects of a single underlying reality. That understanding came late and is held in the deeper records of this branch. What is held openly here are the individual forces as they present themselves to everyday observation and practical use.

Physics underlies everything built in this archive and in this city. The portal network, the energy systems of Nibiru, the crystal technologies -- none of them would exist without the understanding held in these records.
