# 01.03 The Wisdom of What All Things Are Made Of | Keth'vel'shala

*Section compiled by Shala'vel Kemiran, Era 47. Expanded by Vetha'shala Oriskan, Era 103. Final revision by Oma'vel Keriskan, Era 140.*

Everything that exists is made of something. The stone beneath your feet and the air above it and the light falling through it are all made of matter -- of particles held together in patterns of astonishing complexity and variety. Chemistry is the study of those patterns: what the basic constituents of matter are, how they combine into the substances the world is made of, and what happens when different substances meet each other.

The Furlings came to chemistry from two directions simultaneously: from above, as the physical sciences reached small enough scales to ask what matter itself was made of; and from below, as practical craftspeople and healers accumulated detailed knowledge of which materials did what, long before anyone understood why.

The meeting of those two traditions -- the theoretical and the practical -- produced the chemistry held in this branch.

For those who have not yet encountered formal chemistry: the records here progress from the smallest scale (the particles that make up matter) outward to the substances they form, the reactions between them, and the special properties of the materials the Furlings worked with most closely -- particularly the crystal systems that are central to this archive and this city.
