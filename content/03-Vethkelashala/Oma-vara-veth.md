# <span class="furling-pin">Oma'vara'veth</span> | The Four Who Reached Across the Stars Together

> *"We had four of the oldest and most advanced civilisations in multiple galaxies at the same table, sharing knowledge freely, working together, building something. Then each of us faced our hardest moment, and we faced them alone. I do not know if the alliance failed us or if we failed the alliance. I know that it existed. I know what it was. That has to mean something."*
> — Archivist Shela Vorn-Mirath, personal note, Era 146

>> The Four Great Races Alliance brought together the Furlings, the Alterans, the Asgard, and the Nox in a period of shared exploration, knowledge exchange, and cooperative effort. This record covers the alliance at visitor level. The full diplomatic archive — including internal disagreements, treaty texts, and detailed species profiles for the other three races — is held at Student access and above.

## The Four Races

Each of the four races contributed something distinct to the alliance. These were not arbitrary diplomatic categories — they reflected genuine differences in capability, philosophy, and approach that made the combination of all four more capable than any one alone.

**The Furlings** were the educators and mediators. Our contribution to the alliance was described as energy and control of nature — our mastery of geothermal systems, bio-organic technology, and our understanding of how to work with living systems rather than against them. But our deeper contribution was diplomatic. When the Alterans and the Asgard disagreed — which happened — Furling representatives were frequently the ones who found the language that brought both parties back to the table. This role suited us. It also meant we absorbed a disproportionate share of the alliance's friction.

**The Alterans** — known to some species as the Ancients — were the scholars and architects of physical reality. Their understanding of physics, particularly of space, time, and the fundamental forces, exceeded what the other three races had achieved. They built the original portal network that the Furlings later extended across the Kigal Galaxy. Their philosophical trajectory was consistently toward Ascension — toward transcendence of physical existence into a purely energetic state. As this path absorbed more of their attention, their engagement with the alliance diminished. The Furlings maintained closer contact with them than the other two races managed, largely because we shared the role of knowledge-keepers.

**The Asgard** brought technological power and, most distinctively, the study of genetics and biological systems across species. Their capacity for military defence was significant — in the early eras of the alliance they provided protection to the network that the Furlings and Nox could not. They were facing a civilisational crisis of their own at the time of the archive seal: a deterioration in their cloning technology that threatened their continued existence. They did not share the details of this with the alliance. We respected their privacy, and regret that we did not offer more help.

**The Nox** were the most philosophically distinct of the four races. They believed in the absolute principle of non-interference with the natural development of any species — including species under threat. Their relationship with living systems went beyond anything the Furlings had achieved: a direct integration with the natural world that resembled what we only approached through technology. They were the most pacifist members of the alliance, consistently, and they withdrew from any topic that required contemplating violence. We loved them for this and found it frustrating in equal measure.

## What the Alliance Built

Beyond the exchange of knowledge, the alliance produced several shared projects. The most significant was the extension of the Alteran portal network into a galaxy-spanning system, which the Furlings administered and the Asgard protected. The Heliopolis research facility — a shared space for the four races to work together — was the physical centre of the alliance during its most active period.

The alliance also produced the only successful examples of four-way interspecies collaborative art, science, and philosophy. Some of these works are held in the archive; others were lost.

## How the Alliance Faded

The alliance did not formally dissolve. There was no meeting at which it was declared over, no treaty of termination. It simply became less. Each of the four races faced an existential challenge at roughly the same historical moment: the Alterans moved deeper into their Ascension path, the Nox withdrew into their forests, the Keth'alis crisis consumed us entirely, and the Asgard faced their cloning deterioration. The alliance had no framework for this — it had been built for cooperation in peace, not for coordinated survival under pressure.

Whether the alliance could have helped any of the four races had it remained functional is a question our historians discuss with persistent sadness.
