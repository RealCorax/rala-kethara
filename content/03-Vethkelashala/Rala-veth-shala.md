# <span class="furling-pin">Rala'veth'shala</span> | The Wisdom of How Others Speak

<p class="room-subtitle">Languages of Other Species — Every documented tongue, indexed and preserved.</p>
</div>

<div class="room-intro">

Every species that thinks, thinks in language — or in something so close to language that the difference matters less than the similarity. This section holds the linguistic record of every species the Furlings formally documented: grammars, vocabularies, phonologies, writing systems, and the pragmatic patterns that reveal how a species understands the world.

The index at visitor level names every documented language and provides brief notes on its character. Complete records are held at Student access and above. The wrist translation device carries working datasets for the most common languages encountered in the Kigal Galaxy — a practical resource that does not require archive access to use.
