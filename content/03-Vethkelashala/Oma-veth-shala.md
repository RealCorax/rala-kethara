# <span class="furling-pin">Oma'veth'shala</span> | The Wisdom of Making Peace Between Lives

<p class="room-subtitle">Diplomatic Record — How we made contact, what we offered, and what came of it.</p>
</div>

<div class="room-intro">

Every relationship with another species began with a moment of reaching — choosing to make contact, choosing how to do it, choosing what to offer and what to ask. These decisions were never simple. They involved risk, uncertainty, philosophical disagreement, and the awareness that a first contact gone wrong could not be undone. This section holds the record of how we navigated those moments across 150,000 thara'keth of exploration.

Visitor access covers the philosophy and the overview. The detailed records of specific diplomatic missions, treaty texts, and conflict histories are held at deeper access levels.
