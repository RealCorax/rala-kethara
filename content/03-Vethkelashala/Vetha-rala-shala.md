# <span class="furling-pin">Vetha'rala'shala</span> | The Voices of Living Things

<p class="room-subtitle">Species Catalogue — Every documented mind, indexed and profiled.</p>
</div>

<div class="room-intro">

The full species catalogue contains records of every intelligent species the Furlings formally documented across 150,000 thara'keth of exploration. The names and locations of some species are withheld here by deliberate choice — to share their identities openly would be to mark them for those who would do them harm. Their stories are preserved. Their safety, where possible, is also preserved.

What you find at this access level is a selection: the species we knew best, documented most fully, and whose records are safe to share openly. Complete profiles — biology, history, society, and technology — are held at deeper access levels.
