# <span class="furling-pin">Vel'rala'veth</span> | Every Tongue We Have Heard

> *"A language is not a code for meaning. It is a way of cutting the world at its joints. Every species we encountered cut slightly differently. Fourteen thara'keth of translation work taught me that the interesting moment is never the word you can translate — it is the word you cannot."*

>> This index records every language formally documented by the Furling civilisation. Brief notes appear here. Complete records — grammar, vocabulary, phonology, writing systems — are held at Student access. The wrist translation device you carry contains working datasets for the most commonly encountered languages in this region.

## On the Wrist Device and Translation

The wrist device carries working translation datasets for the languages most likely to be encountered in the Kigal Galaxy's currently accessible regions: Furling, Keshari Trade, Aethen Standard, and a partial dataset for Vethari Classical. Encounter with other species may require downloading additional datasets if they are available, or reverting to the device's structural-pattern analysis mode, which provides approximate meaning without nuance.

Structural-pattern analysis works reasonably well for verbal languages with subject-object-verb ordering similar to the most common humanoid types. It works poorly for Moravel, Lúmenari, Nahari, and Mirathi. Attempting to have a sensitive diplomatic conversation through structural-pattern analysis with a Velhari equivalent would be inadvisable. This is noted because someone will eventually try.
