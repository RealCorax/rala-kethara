# <span class="furling-pin">Oma'vel'athela</span> | The Council of 3 — How We Govern Ourselves

> *"The Council system was not Tharúven's first proposal. His first proposal was simpler. The survivors of the Great War rejected it, because it placed too much in a single pair of hands. His second proposal, which became what we have now, answered that objection. He was pleased to have been corrected."*

>> This record covers the Council of 3 governance system — its origins, its structure, its principles, and why it took the form it did. For the philosophy behind it, see the companion record on the Kora'thesh Veshúnar.

## The Origin

The Council of 3 was designed by [[Tharúven Kora'thesh]] in the Reconstruction Period following the Great War. Its specific form — three rotating members rather than any other number — emerged from a particular lesson of the tribal period: that structures built around the authority of a single individual collapse when that individual is gone. And that structures built around two create permanent opposition.

Three provides a working majority without creating a permanent majority. Three cannot deadlock the way two can. Three is not a single voice that silences others. The reasoning was specific and it was tested, in the debates of the Reconstruction Period, before it was adopted.

Tharúven himself refused to serve on the first Council. He said that any structure that depended on a specific person was already a failed structure.

## How It Works

Every Furling settlement of significant size is governed by a Council of 3. Positions are not permanent. They are rotated among qualified members of the community so that no individual or group can consolidate authority over time. The definition of "qualified" has been debated across the eras and varies somewhat by world and era, but the core criteria have been consistent: demonstrated competence, demonstrated commitment to the Kora'thesh Veshúnar, and demonstrated willingness to give up the position when the rotation requires it.

The Head Council of 3, which oversees all the Councils of 3 across the galaxy, is based on [[Mira]]. Its authority is coordinating rather than commanding — it does not override local councils except in defined circumstances involving the welfare of other settlements.

Decisions within a Council are reached by discussion until a majority position emerges. Dissenting positions are recorded. The archive holds Council records from across all eras, and they are studied as both history and as examples of how difficult questions get worked through.

## What It Cannot Do

The Council system has explicit limits, derived directly from the Kora'thesh Veshúnar.

A Council cannot make permanent what should be temporary. Governance is a service, not a possession. A Council cannot act to extend its own authority or existence beyond its designated term.

A Council cannot act against the basic welfare of those it serves, as defined by the Kora'thesh Veshúnar. There is no mechanism by which a majority of three can legitimately authorise harm to the community it exists to serve. Records of Councils that attempted this exist in the archive as cautionary history.

A Council cannot silence dissent. Minority positions are recorded. Disagreement is not disloyalty.

## Citizenship and Standing

All Furlings are equal in rights and responsibilities under the Council system. Non-Furlings visiting or living in a Furling settlement may gain access to knowledge, support, and community participation through a process assessed by the local Council, but cannot hold Council positions. This is not a judgment about the worth of other species — it is a recognition that the Council system grows from the Kora'thesh Veshúnar, which is specifically a Furling identity document.

Status and respect within the community are earned through contribution and wisdom. Birth carries no privilege beyond the welcome given to every kinara.
