# <span class="furling-pin">Bulutha</span> | Keeper of Nibiru

> *"She went to a small world in another galaxy, to a people who did not know there were other people, and she taught them what she knew. She stayed long enough that some of them probably thought of her as one of them. She left before she became a legend. That was characteristic of her."*
> — personal correspondence, compiler unknown, Era ~147

## The Name

<span class="furling-pin">Bulutha</span> — life-sustainer. The name is both description and aspiration. Those who knew her in her working life describe someone who understood the second principle of the Kora'thesh Veshúnar not as an abstract commitment but as a daily practice: what sustains the Whole must never be withheld.

## The Mesopotamia Visit

Following the loss of the Thevathal settlement in the Milky Way, Bulutha travelled to the planet of the humans — specifically to the region called Mesopotamia in the local language — and spent time among the human populations there.

What she taught: writing systems that influenced the development of the notation these people were already developing; refinements to agricultural practice; concepts of justice and communal law that would echo through their subsequent legal traditions; methods of astronomical observation. The record at this access level does not contain the full account of her time there. What is documented is that she taught genuine things, taught them carefully, and remained long enough to see that they had taken root.

Eighteen humans were selected by Bulutha for further learning and taken through a Stargate. What they were taught, where they went, and what became of them is not in any record accessible at Visitor level.

## The Testament

Before leaving, Bulutha created a tablet. On its surface it reads as a record of her time teaching the Mesopotamians — a gift of documentation for a people who were developing their written records and would benefit from the additional account. Encoded within its structure, invisible to those who did not know to look, is a portal address.

The address leads to this city.

Whether this was an act of hope — a message left for a species that might one day be ready to find what we left behind — or something more specific, the record does not say clearly. Bulutha's personal memory crystal, held at Keeper access, may contain a fuller account. The summary record available at this level does not.

## The Final Record

Bulutha is listed as the last Keeper of this archive before the departure. The final log entry before the portal closed is attributed to a colleague. Bulutha's own last recorded words are in the memory crystal.

The archive is here because she stayed long enough to ensure it would be.
