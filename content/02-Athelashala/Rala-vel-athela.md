<h1><span class="furling-pin">Rala'vel'athela</span> | The Living Voice of Our Tongue</h1>
<p class="rh-description">The Furling language in full. Begin here if you have just arrived and need to understand what you are reading. The grammar, the number system, the roots from which all words are built — these are documented for any species that wishes to move through the archive in Furling rather than in translation. The language is not separate from the philosophy. Once you see how the grammar works, you will find you already understand something about us that no amount of explanation could convey.</p>
