# <span class="furling-pin">Vetha'shora Oun-Mira</span> | Biologist, Era 129

Vetha'shora Oun-Mira was the lead biologist in the archive's Furling biology documentation project in the late eras. Her principal work was the systematic description of Furling physical biology, the vel'shora system, and the medical practices held in sections 02.01 and cross-referenced in Division 04.

Born on the colony world Keth'avar, she came to Nibiru as a young adult and remained for the rest of her career. Her observation of visiting species contributed significantly to the archive's cross-species medical records.

Her biology records are at Visitor and Student access. Her personal journals are held at Student access.
