# <span class="furling-pin">Miravel Oun-Nai</span> | Linguist, Era 134

> *"She said once that she had spent forty thara'keth studying our language and was still not certain she spoke it well. Everyone who heard her say this found it slightly implausible."*

Miravel Oun-Nai was the lead linguist of the Rala'Kethara Archive's language division in the final eras before the departure. Her principal contribution was the consolidation of the Modern High Furling reference — the definitive grammar, phonology, and vocabulary record that forms the primary language documentation in section 02.07.

She was born on Mira and spent most of her working life attached to the archive at Nibiru, where she was known for unusual patience with visiting species attempting to learn the language and for an equally unusual willingness to be corrected in her conclusions by evidence she had not previously considered.

Her language records are at Visitor access throughout. Her personal journals are held at Student access.
