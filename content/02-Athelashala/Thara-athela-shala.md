<h1><span class="furling-pin">Thara'athela'shala</span> | The Cycles of What We Were</h1>
<p class="rh-description">Our history. From the archaeological record of our earliest ancestors through the Great War, the long expansion across the Kigal Galaxy, the Golden Age, the contact with the Athel'kin, and what followed from it. Some of this history is held carefully. The fact of it is not hidden. We do not study our history because it is comfortable. We study it because it is ours.</p>
