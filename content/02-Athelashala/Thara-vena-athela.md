# <span class="furling-pin">Thara'vena'athela</span> | The Exodus — What We Left Behind

> *"The city is sealed. The archive is intact. Everything we were is in here. We are leaving so that it does not end."*
> — Vel'oma Shala-Theth, Era 141, final log before the portal closed

>> This record covers the Furling exodus from the Kigal Galaxy. What led to it the discovery of what the Athel'kin had become, and the war that followed is held at Deep Circle access. This record covers the fact and the nature of the departure: why we left the way we left, what was left behind, and what that means for those who encounter this archive now.

## The War and Its End

For those at Visitor access: a war came. The Athel'kin, transformed by the consequences of the Vetha'meva into something we had not intended and could not have foreseen, became a force that moved through the Kigal Galaxy in a way that Furling civilisation ultimately could not withstand. The other members of the Four Great Races Alliance were unavailable: the Alterans had ascended or were ascending; the Asgard were engaged with their own existential conflict; the Nox held to their principle of non-interference.

The Furlings fought. For many decades across many worlds, we fought. We were effective. We were not, in the end, sufficient. World after world fell. The populations remaining on those worlds came under the control of those who had once been the Athel'kin.

The full account of the war and of the attempts at resolution that preceded it is held at Deep Circle access. It is held there not because it is shameful it is our history and we do not hide our history but because understanding it fully requires the foundation of knowing who the Athel'kin were before and what the Vetha'meva was. Those records are the necessary ground.

## The Departure

The decision to leave was not made lightly or quickly. The evacuation of Furling populations through the portal network to the Milky Way galaxy took place across the final phase of the war. It was not a retreat in the military sense — it was an organised departure of a civilisation that understood it could not hold its home.

Many things were carried through the portals. The archive was already here, in this city, intact. Knowledge was carried in crystal. People were carried in whatever ships remained.

Many things were left. Some could not be moved. Some were left deliberately — trusted to the durability of what we built, and to the hope that someone might eventually find what we left behind.

This city was not sealed, locked, or protected against discovery. It was simply abandoned in place. No one who knew where it was remained to tell anyone where it was. It survived because it was hidden by the fact that no one knew to look for it.

## What Remains

Everything that is in this archive is what we were. The Rala'Kethara was named Voice-Eternal not as an aspiration toward our own immortality but as a description of what knowledge is when it is cared for: it keeps speaking after the speaker is gone.

The archive does not know what has happened in the Kigal Galaxy since we left. Selavai has maintained it, but she has not added to it. The records end at the point of departure. What has occurred since is not here because there was no one here to record it.

If you are reading this, something has changed. The archive is open to new voices. The founding document explains how.
