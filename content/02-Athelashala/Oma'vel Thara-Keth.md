# <span class="furling-pin">Oma'vel Thara-Keth</span> | Governance Scholar, Era 131

Oma'vel Thara-Keth compiled the governance and society records in section 02.03. Her specialisation was the Oma'vel'athela or Council of 3 system, its historical development, its practical operation across different worlds, and its philosophical relationship to the Kora'thesh Veshúnar.

She served two terms on the planetary Council of 3 during her career, which she described as *"the most useful research I ever did."*

Her governance records are at Visitor access. Her Council journals from her terms of service are held at Keeper access.
