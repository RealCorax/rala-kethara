# <span class="furling-pin">Tharúven Kora'thesh</span> | The Unifier

> *"He refused to serve on the first Council himself. He said that any structure that depended on a specific person was already a failed structure. We have spent the entire history of our civilisation trying to be worthy of that refusal."*

## Who He Was Before

Tharúven Kora'thesh was not a figure of pre-war significance. He held no tribal leadership position. The surviving accounts — fragmentary, copied multiple times from oral sources — describe him as a soldier in the most literal sense: a Furling who had been fighting since he was old enough to hold a weapon, not from ideology but because that was what survival in his region required.

He was, by his own account in the fragments that survive, unremarkable before the war ended. He fought. He kept himself alive. He kept those nearest to him alive when he could. He made no speeches. He led no movements. He was one among many hundreds of thousands of Furlings who endured the Great War.

## What He Did After

What happened after the war's exhaustion is documented with moderate confidence — the earliest written records are from this period, though most originals were lost and the surviving texts are copies made in Eras 9 to 15.

Tharúven began travelling between the surviving communities of Mira. He was not sent by anyone. There was no authority left to send him. He went because he understood something that took others longer to see: that the survivors were about to repeat the conditions that had produced the war, because they had not yet built anything different.

He listened before he spoke. He proposed rather than commanded. He was, in every account, constitutionally opposed to placing authority in himself. When communities offered him leadership, he redirected the offer toward structures. When his name was put forward for the first Council, he declined explicitly and on the record.

The result of his work was the [[Kora'thesh Veshúnar]] — the Five Principles — and the Council of 3 governance system. He created both in the immediate post-war period, in dialogue with survivors across the devastated surface of Mira, with fewer than one million Furlings remaining alive.

## His Later Life

Tharúven Kora'thesh spent the rest of his life as an ordinary citizen of the Reconstruction. He helped where he was needed. He spoke when he was asked. He built nothing official after the founding work was done.

He died in Era 7, by natural causes, at an estimated age of 90 thara'keth. His grave on Mira is marked with a simple crystal-slate bearing the opening words of the Kora'thesh Veshúnar. By his explicit instruction, it bears no title.

The most often quoted line from the fragments of his personal record:

*"I want what we build to be so good that it does not need me. If it needs me, I have not done my work."*
