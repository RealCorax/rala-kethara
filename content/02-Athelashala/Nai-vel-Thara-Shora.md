# <span class="furling-pin">Nai'vel Thara-Shora</span> | Cultural Recorder, Era 138

Nai'vel Thara-Shora compiled the daily life and material culture records that form section 02.05. Her approach was distinctive: she wrote from inside daily life rather than from scholarly distance, and her records consequently carry an immediacy that more formally structured accounts sometimes lack.

She described her work as *"trying to say what Tuesday feels like, not just what it is."*

Her cultural records are at Visitor access. Her personal journals, which extend the same sensibility into more private material, are held at Student access.
