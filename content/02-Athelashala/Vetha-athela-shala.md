<h1><span class="furling-pin">Vetha'athela'shala</span> | The Wisdom of How We First Lived</h1>
<p class="rh-description">Furling origins and biology. Where we came from, what our bodies are, how we grow and age across lives that span centuries. The physical form of the Furling people — our appearance, our senses, our life stages, the vel'shora marks that make each of us legible to every other — is documented here as precisely as we could manage. We found it surprisingly difficult to write about ourselves without writing about everything we valued at the same time.</p>
