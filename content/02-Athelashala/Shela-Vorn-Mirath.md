# <span class="furling-pin">Shela Vorn-Mirath</span> | Chief Archivist, Era 134

> *"We do not study our history because it is comfortable. We study it because it is ours — the beautiful and the shameful alike."*
> — Preface to the Fourth Consolidated Edition of the Historical Record

Shela Vorn-Mirath served as Chief Archivist of the Rala'Kethara from Era 128 through to the departure. She was responsible for the final major revision of the historical record — the Fourth Consolidated Edition that forms the primary historical documentation in section 02.02 — as well as the archive's structural documentation in section 08.

She was known in the scholarly community for a particular combination of intellectual rigour and genuine warmth: she held high standards for evidence while being genuinely interested in the people behind every record, including those whose conclusions she disagreed with. Several of her most significant contributions to historical scholarship are the moments in which she explicitly acknowledged the limits of what could be known, rather than papering over them with confident assertion.

Her personal memoir, covering her time as Chief Archivist, is held at Student access. Her correspondence with colleagues across the final eras is held at Student to Citizen access.
