# <span class="furling-pin">Shala'vel Oun-Thara</span> | Philosopher, Era 142

Shala'vel Oun-Thara was the archive's principal philosopher in the final years before the departure. Her work covers the philosophy of knowledge, the practical ethics of the Kora'thesh Veshúnar, and the spirituality records in section 02.04 and 02.05.

She was known for an ability to write about difficult philosophical positions with clarity that made them accessible without making them seem simpler than they were. Her records on the ethics of knowledge withholding are considered some of the most important philosophical documents compiled in the archive's final era.

Her philosophical records are at Visitor and Student access. Her personal philosophical notebooks are held at Student access.
