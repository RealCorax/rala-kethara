# <span class="furling-pin">Thevan'skela'vel</span> | A First Journey Through

> *This guide was written for visitors to Nibiru who have been given access to the portal network before they feel entirely comfortable with the idea. You may have used a Stargate before, or nothing like it at all. Either way, what follows is honest, practical, and written by someone who remembers very clearly what it felt like the first time.*
> — Reva Naleth, Era 143

>> This is a practical step-by-step guide to travelling through a Furling portal for the first time. It covers preparation, dialling, crossing, and what to do when you arrive. Read the companion record on network operations for the fuller history and engineering context.

## Before You Dial

**Check that you have an address.** A portal address is seven symbols: six for the destination, one for your origin point. You need to know all six destination symbols before you begin dialling. The origin symbol is already set for the portal you are standing at — you do not need to enter it. If you are using a wrist device, your addresses are stored in its crystal memory; the device will confirm which addresses it holds.

**Check that your key is functional.** If you are dialling by hand, insert the key into the socket at the right side of the portal frame. You should hear — and feel — a faint resonance as the portal's power draw activates and the inner ring of twelve symbols becomes visible. If nothing happens, the key and the portal may not be matched. Not every key works with every portal. The key that opens Nibiru's surface portal is specific to that installation.

**Check that someone knows where you are going.** This is not a formality. The portal network spans the Kigal Galaxy. Most of it has not been actively monitored for four thousand thara'keth. We do not have a current record of which portals remain active. An address that was valid when this archive was sealed may lead to a portal that has lost power, or to a world that has changed significantly. Tell someone your destination and your expected return before you dial.

## Dialling

With the key inserted and the inner ring visible:

1. Rotate the key until its tip points to your first destination symbol. Press the key gently inward until you feel it lock. The symbol will register.

2. If your address includes symbols from the middle ring, press the first side button on the key. Eleven more symbols will appear holographically around the inner ring.

3. If your address includes symbols from the outer ring, press the second side button. Twelve more symbols appear beyond the middle ring.

4. Continue rotating and locking each symbol in your six-symbol sequence. Take your time. Rushing does not speed the process and increases the chance of error.

5. After the sixth symbol is locked, pull the key outward.

If the address is valid and the destination portal is active, the wormhole will engage. You will see the event horizon form: a circular field, initially turbulent, that settles within a few moments into something that looks like a vertical pool of light. Do not step through during the initial turbulence. Wait until it settles.

If nothing happens after you pull the key, the destination portal is either inactive or the address was incorrect. You can attempt again. Pulling the key without a completed address or with an invalid sequence carries no risk — the portal simply does not activate.

**Wrist device dialling:** If you are using a wrist device rather than a physical key, approach the portal within arm's reach, select your destination address from the device's memory, and confirm. The device transmits the dialling sequence directly to the portal's crystal network. The process is otherwise identical.


## Arriving

You will emerge through the destination portal. Look at your surroundings before stepping away from the portal, and move away from the arrival point immediately so that others can emerge behind you.

Furling portals have no arrival sickness equivalent to that reported by some Stargate users. The transit system was calibrated across many generations of use by many different species. If you feel briefly unsteady, sit down. It passes quickly.

**Returning:** The destination portal can be dialled for return travel if it has a key present or if your wrist device is within range. If you are travelling to a portal you are unfamiliar with, check before departure whether a key exists at the destination or whether your wrist device will be sufficient. Some installations in the network were maintained-portal-only, with no key available on arrival.
