# <span class="furling-pin">Vel Omathis</span> | Engineer and Teacher of Living Systems

> [!person-portrait]
> Era 88–148 · Velakris Station, then Nibiru · Bio-Organic Engineering, Applied Biology

## Life and Work

Vel Omathis was not born on Mira. They came into the world at the Velakris research station — the orbital facility positioned in the asteroid belt between Mira and the outer system — where their parent cohort was completing a long-cycle project studying the behaviour of keth'vetha'mira under high-vacuum conditions. They were the first Furling to be born off-world in their generation, a distinction they found moderately interesting and occasionally useful for opening conversations.

Their upbringing between Velakris station and Mira gave them an early and practical familiarity with sealed environmental systems: what it means to keep air breathable, water clean, and walls intact when the outside is trying to kill everything inside. This would shape their entire career.

Vel trained as an engineer in the applied biology track, which at the time was considered the more difficult and less prestigious branch of bio-organic technology. The prestigious track was architectural — building living walls, cultivating city structures. Applied biology dealt with the smaller, messier, harder-to-explain work: how you keep a living system functional when conditions change faster than the system can adapt, how you maintain the mycelial networks inside a structure that is six hundred thara'keth old and behaving unexpectedly, how you explain to a team that the problem is the wall is not getting enough light and the solution is to move a window.

They relocated to Nibiru in Era 122, at the invitation of the city's maintenance council, and spent the remaining twenty-six thara'keth of their life working as both an engineer and an archivist. The record compiled for section 04.01.02 was completed in Era 131, when Vel was 43 thara'keth old, as part of a broader effort to ensure that the visitor-level technology documentation reflected what visitors actually needed to know. They were, by all accounts, an extremely good teacher — patient with confusion, direct about complexity, and genuinely delighted by the moment when a student understood something they had previously thought impenetrable.

Vel died in Era 148, two thara'keth before the exodus. They had been aware, for several thara'keth before their death, that the city would eventually need to be left. They chose to remain in Nibiru during those final years rather than relocate, and spent their last thara'keth ensuring that the bio-organic systems of the city were as self-sustaining as they could be made.

The atmospheric systems that still function today are partly a product of work Vel did in Era 144–146 to improve their long-cycle resilience.

> [!arc-note] On the Maintenance Log
> Vel Omathis's personal log of Nibiru's systems in the final years before the exodus is one of the most detailed technical records of the city's infrastructure available in the archive. It is held at Keeper access not because it contains classified material, but because it requires a substantial technical foundation to use correctly. Misapplied, it could lead to interventions that cause more harm than the problem they are trying to solve.
