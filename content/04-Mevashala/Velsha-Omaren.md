# <span class="furling-pin">Velsha Omaren</span> | Healer of Minds and Teacher of Healers

> [!person-portrait]
> Era 84–147 · Mira, Shala'vel Institute · Mental Health, Trauma Medicine, Healer Support

## Life and Work

Velsha Omaren came to healing through an unusual route: they began their working life as a community mediator in one of Mira's larger cities, helping to resolve disputes and manage the kinds of interpersonal ruptures that, in any community, produce ongoing harm if left unaddressed. They were good at this work, but became increasingly aware that many of the situations they were called to mediate had psychological injury at their root — grief, trauma, accumulated stress, experiences that had not been processed — that no amount of formal mediation could resolve because the problem was not a disagreement but a wound.

At twenty-eight thara'keth, considerably later than most Furlings began formal study, Velsha entered medical training. They focused entirely on psychological medicine, which was, in that period, still working to establish itself as equal in importance to physical medicine within Furling healing practice. Velsha was one of the principal advocates for this parity across their career, and the argument they made — that separating physical and psychological medicine produces worse outcomes for both — appears in multiple forms in their archive contributions, including the visitor-level record for this section.

Their most significant practical contribution was to healer support: the recognition that healers, who spend their working lives in sustained contact with suffering, require the same quality of care they give to their patients, and that failing to provide this produces burnout, error, and eventual harm to the patients healers are trying to help. Their work in Era 115 establishing the healer support protocols used in Nibiru's medical facility is documented at Student access.

Velsha worked at Nibiru from Era 119 to the exodus, remaining among the last to leave. They survived the transit and their subsequent fate is not recorded in the archive, as the record ends at Era 147.

## A Note on the Word Shola'keth

The Furling word <span class="furling-pin-inline">shola'keth</span> — "held without end" — appears in the opening of the mental health record. Velsha coined it in Era 109 during a teaching session, reaching for something that described the specific state of a person who has been carrying grief or trauma so long that the weight has become the baseline. The word entered common usage within the healing community within a generation. It is now standard vocabulary.
