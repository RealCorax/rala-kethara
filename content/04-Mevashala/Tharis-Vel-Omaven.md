# <span class="furling-pin">Tharis Vel-Omaven</span> | Engineer of Energy Systems

> [!person-portrait]
> Era 68–134 · Mira, then Nibiru · Geothermal Engineering, Energy Architecture

## Life and Work

Tharis Vel-Omaven grew up in the mining settlements of Mira's southern continent, where the deep crystal-tap operations that supplied much of the homeworld's energy infrastructure were based. Their parent-cohort included two tap engineers and a geological surveyor, and Tharis spent their childhood understanding, before they had words for it, that energy was something that came from inside the world rather than from outside it.

This early understanding shaped everything. When Tharis entered formal study at eighteen thara'keth, they went directly into geothermal engineering — not the more celebrated field of stellar energy collection, which drew larger cohorts in that era, but the older, quieter discipline of listening to what worlds generated internally and learning to work with it. Their early research focused on long-cycle tap stability: how to design a geothermal extraction system that would remain productive across thousands rather than hundreds of thara'keth, without requiring the continuous recalibration that most systems of the period needed.

The answer they developed in Era 95 — distributed tap networks with autonomous load-balancing crystal arrays — became the standard for all major Furling installations built after that date. Nibiru's geothermal system uses this architecture. The fact that it is still running at approximately seventy to eighty percent capacity over four thousand thara'keth after the exodus is a direct consequence of Tharis's design work, though Tharis died in Era 134 and never knew it would be tested in this way.

Tharis came to Nibiru in Era 119, invited to consult on the micro-sun system, which had been experiencing slow drift in its resonance calibration. They stayed for fifteen thara'keth, overhauling both the micro-sun resonance array and several sections of the city's geothermal tap network. The visitor-level energy record for section 04.01.04 was compiled during this period, at the request of the archive's education division.

Colleagues describe Tharis as methodical, precise, and entirely uninterested in reputation. Their personal records note only technical observations, never social commentary. The one exception is a single line in a journal from Era 131: *"Nibiru will outlast all of us. This is as it should be."*
