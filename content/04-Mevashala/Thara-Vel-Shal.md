> [!person-portrait]
> Era 64–131 · Mira, farming settlements · Agriculture, Ecosystem Design, Nibiru Farm Systems

## Life and Work

Thara Vel-Shal spent the first thirty-five thara'keth of their life on Mira's farming settlements, in the broad river valleys of the northern continent where some of the oldest continuously cultivated growing systems on the homeworld operated. They did not come from an academic background. Their parents and grandparents had been growers; Thara learned the work by doing it before they learned to describe it in technical terms.

The decision to enter formal study came relatively late, at thirty-three thara'keth, prompted by a specific problem: the growing system their family had maintained for several generations was behaving unexpectedly, producing declining yields despite apparently unchanged conditions, and Thara had exhausted the practical knowledge available to them locally. The university could not, it turned out, immediately explain the problem either. But the investigation produced a research direction — the long-cycle dynamics of cultivated ecosystems under apparent stability — that became the foundation of Thara's academic career.

Their contribution was to the understanding of what happens to designed growing systems over very long periods: how they drift from their original state, what drives that drift, how to distinguish drift that is benign from drift that is harmful, and how to intervene when necessary without disrupting the functioning parts of the system. This was, at the time, a relatively neglected area of research. Most agricultural science focused on establishing and optimising systems rather than on their long-term behaviour. Thara's work changed that emphasis.

They came to Nibiru in Era 90, invited to consult on the farm district's design for extended unattended operation. The Thara'kela systems were already partially built; Thara's contribution was to the long-cycle resilience features that allowed the district to continue functioning without maintenance. The visitor-level archive records for this section were compiled later, during a second residency in Era 95–102.

Thara died in Era 131, at sixty-seven thara'keth, on Mira. They had returned to the homeworld in Era 110, spending their final thara'keth documenting the agricultural systems of the northern settlements they had grown up in. That documentation, compiled with the same care they brought to all their work, is in the archive at Student access.
