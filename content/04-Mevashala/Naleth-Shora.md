# <span class="furling-pin">Naleth Shora</span> | Linguist and Communication Engineer

> [!person-portrait]
> Era 97–148 · Velakris Station, then Mira, then Nibiru · Communication Technology, Xenolinguistics

## Life and Work

Naleth Shora was, by their own description in several interviews preserved in the archive, someone who had never been comfortable with misunderstanding. Not moral misunderstanding, which they considered navigable — factual misunderstanding, the kind that arises when two minds cannot find shared language for the same experience. It troubled them in a way that others found slightly intense and that produced, across a career of over sixty thara'keth, some of the most significant advances in Furling translation technology of the late era.

They were born at Velakris Station and came to Mira at age twelve to study, initially in theoretical linguistics, then increasingly in the applied engineering of communication systems. The shift happened when they were assigned, at twenty-three thara'keth, to a first-contact support team working with a newly encountered species in the mid-galaxy region. The communication failures during that encounter — not hostile, simply structural, two minds trying to reach each other through systems that had not been designed with either of them in mind — became the organising problem of the rest of their career.

Naleth's core contribution was to the wrist device's translation architecture: the multi-layered dataset system that holds working models of multiple language structures simultaneously and mediates between them fast enough to function in real conversation. Prior to their work, translation via wrist device had a delay that made it usable for deliberate exchanges but not for natural conversation. The calibration system they developed in Era 122, which allows the device to adapt its model to a specific user's speech patterns over days of use, reduced that delay to the point where most users stopped noticing it.

They came to Nibiru in Era 131 and remained for the rest of their life, working on the archive's communication interfaces and on the dataset expansion project, which aimed to add translation capacity for every documented species in the archive before the city was closed. The dataset was not completed. What was finished is substantial, and what was left unfinished is documented in a working notes file at Student access.

Naleth died in Era 148, shortly before the exodus. They had been unwell for several thara'keth and chose not to leave early, completing the final additions to the communication dataset until they were no longer able to work.

## A Note on the Wrist Device in Use

Naleth Shora was aware that the devices they had devoted their career to would eventually be used by people they would never meet, encountering species they had never documented, in conversations whose content they could not anticipate. Their final note in the working file reads: *"The device will do what it can. For the rest: listen carefully, speak slowly, and assume good intent until the evidence is clear."*
