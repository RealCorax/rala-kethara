# <span class="furling-pin">Rala'skela'thael</span> | The Star-Cycle-Marks

> *Varathal named the first twelve symbols in Era 14, working from the stellar survey data that had just come back from the outer reaches of the Kigal Galaxy. The choice to use sounds rooted in the language rather than arbitrary notation was not unanimous among the engineers. Varathal's position was simple: we are building something that will outlast everyone who builds it. It should speak.*
> — Archive note, compiler unknown, Era 19

>> The thirty-five symbols of the Furling portal network. Each corresponds to a stellar formation visible from the core of the Kigal Galaxy. Each sound is drawn from the roots of the Furling language. An address is six of these sounds spoken in sequence. The seventh sound identifies the portal you are dialling from.

## How to Read This Reference

The three rings of a portal dial each contain a subset of the symbols. The inner ring activates when the key is inserted. The middle and outer rings appear holographically when the key's side buttons are pressed in sequence.

Each symbol's **sound** is spoken when dialling from memory. The **root echo** shows the Furling language root each sound was drawn from. The **meaning carried** is what a Furling hears beneath the navigation code — not required for dialling, but present in every address spoken aloud.
